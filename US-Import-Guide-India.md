# US Products Worth Importing to India — A ₹0–30,000 Buyer's Guide

*Quality toys (adult), collectibles, watches, Pokémon, anime, Kindle, books & electronics that are
common in the USA but hard to get in India — with realistic landed-cost-in-India pricing.*

> Compiled mid-2026. FX assumed ≈ **₹83 = $1**. Prices, stock and customs rules change constantly —
> treat every ₹ figure as an **estimate**, not a quote.

---

## TL;DR — How to read this guide

**Landed cost = US price + international shipping + Indian customs duty + GST.** The duty part swings
*wildly* by product category, and that — not the US sticker price — decides whether importing is smart.

| Category (HSN chapter) | Indian import duty (approx) | Real landed markup over US price |
|---|---|---|
| **Books / printed matter** (Ch. 49) | **0% — duty-free** | **~+10%** (just shipping) ✅ best value |
| **Electronics / e-readers** (Ch. 85) | ~10% BCD + 10% SWS + 18% IGST | **~+30%** ✅ good |
| **Watches** (Ch. 91) | ~20% BCD + 18% IGST | ~+45–55% ⚠️ moderate |
| **Toys / figures / collectibles / TCG** (Ch. 95) | ~60–70% BCD + 10% SWS + 18% IGST | **~+85–90% (nearly 2×)** ❌ + BIS toy-cert risk |

**So, the honest framing:**

- 📚 **Books & e-readers** are the *best-value* imports — low/zero duty, and many titles/models simply
  aren't sold in India.
- 🎮 **Collectibles, Funko, Pokémon/MTG cards, anime figures** are the *most exclusive* but carry the
  *heaviest* duty. Import them for **access**, not savings — expect roughly **2× the US price** delivered,
  plus a real risk of **BIS (toy-safety) customs detention** on figures and plush.
- 🛑 A few "US-exclusive" things are now sold in India at or below import cost — those are flagged
  **"Don't bother importing"** so you don't waste money.

Each item below lists: **US$ price**, **straight ₹ conversion**, **estimated landed ₹ in India**,
**why it's hard to get here**, **where to buy / does it ship to India**, and **quality notes**.

## 1. Collectibles (adult collectors)

**Why hard in India:** No official Funko/Kidrobot/Topps/Wizards retail presence; US store-exclusives
never reach Indian shelves; local grey-market markups run 100–300%. **Customs is brutal here (Ch. 95,
~85–90% all-in)** and toys can be flagged for **BIS IS 9873** certification.

| Product | US price | ₹ (straight) | Est. landed ₹ | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Funko Pop store exclusives** (GameStop / Target / Funko Shop) | $15–25 | ₹1,250–2,100 | **₹2,500–4,000** | The whole point is the *exclusive* sticker; none ship to India directly — use a forwarder. |
| **Vaulted / chase Funko Pops** (secondary) | $30–60 | ₹2,500–5,000 | ₹5,000–9,000 | eBay; buy graded/authenticated for chases. |
| **Kidrobot designer vinyl** (Dunny, blind boxes) | $20–45 | ₹1,650–3,700 | ₹3,500–6,500 | Street-art toy culture absent in India; some international shipping. |
| **Superplastic** (Janky, Guggimon, SuperKranky) | $99–290 | ₹8,200–24,000 | ₹15,000–28,000+ | Premium art toys; high duty — at the top of budget once landed. |
| **Enamel pin sets** (Kickstarter / artist collabs) | $25–60/set | ₹2,000–5,000 | ₹3,500–7,000 | Light & cheap to ship; Etsy/Kickstarter creators often ship intl. |
| **Sports cards — Topps (MLB) / Panini (NFL) mega boxes** | $50–70 | ₹4,150–5,800 | **₹7,000–9,000** | Region-locked licensing; not sold in India. eBay ships select listings. |
| **MTG sealed** — Bundle/Fat Pack | $40–50 | ₹3,300–4,150 | ₹6,000–8,000 | No WotC India presence; lighter than a booster box = cheaper to forward. |
| **MTG sealed** — Draft/Set Booster Box (36) | $130–150 | ₹10,800–12,500 | **₹18,000–21,000** | Great per-pack value vs Indian grey market. |

**Where Americans buy:** gamestop.com, target.com, funko.com, kidrobot.com, superplastic.co,
cardkingdom.com, topps.com, eBay. **Ships to India?** Mostly **no direct** — use a forwarder (see §9) or
eBay Global Shipping.

Continental US, Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, US Virgin Islands & Minor Outlying Islands). There is **no
official Pokémon distributor in India** — local sellers mark up sealed product 200–300% or import grey.
Same heavy Ch. 95 duty applies.

## 8. Books (the smart, duty-free import)

📚 **Books are essentially duty-free in India** (Ch. 49), so the only real add-on is airmail (~+10%).
*Caveat:* mass-market titles usually have **cheaper Indian editions** — import only for **editions that
don't exist here** (special/illustrated/signed/imports).

## 9. Import logistics for India

**Package forwarders** (they give you a free US address, then reship to India; consolidate multiple buys
into one box to save):

| Service | Notes |
|---|---|
| **MyUS** | Most popular for India; consolidation ~$3/pkg; weight-based rates; no US sales tax on exports. |
| **Stackry** | Free address; ~$20 for 1–3 lb; holds packages up to 60 days; good consolidation. |
| **ShipToBox** | No membership fee; "buy-for-me" service for stores that won't sell to forwarders. |
| **Borderlinx** | Pre-calculates duties upfront; good for heavier parcels. |
| **PPOBox / Forward2Me** | Handle both US **and** UK → India forwarding. |

**Other routes:**
- **Amazon Global Store (on Amazon.in):** Amazon handles customs; all-in price shown upfront via an
  "Import Fees Deposit" (often over-estimated; refunded if actual duty is lower). Convenient but pricier,
  and **Kindles are *not* on Global Store to India**.
- **eBay Global Shipping Program:** duties pre-calculated, buyer protection kept — good for collectibles.
- **USPS airmail:** best for **books** (duty-free, ~$8–25).

**Duty & rules (2026):**
- **2026 baggage allowance:** resident Indians ₹75,000 duty-free when *carrying* goods (e.g. a friend
  flying in); **laptops one-per-adult duty-free**. (This is *baggage*, separate from courier imports.)
- **Couriered personal imports** are assessed by HSN: books ~0%, electronics ~10% BCD + 10% SWS + 18%
  IGST, watches ~20% BCD + 18% IGST, **toys/collectibles ~60–70% BCD + 10% SWS + 18% IGST**.
- **BIS IS 9873 (toy safety):** physical toys/figures/plush can be **detained** at customs without
  certification. Buy from vendors who pre-certify, or accept the detention risk.

**Worked landed-cost examples:**
- *Kindle Colorsoft* — $180 sale + $18 ship ≈ ₹16,400 CIF → +10% BCD +SWS +18% IGST ≈ **₹21,400 landed.**
- *Folio Society Don Quixote* — $65 + $8 airmail ≈ ₹6,050 CIF → **0% duty → ₹6,050 landed** (vs ₹8–10k local).
- *Pokémon ETB* — $50 ≈ ₹4,150 CIF → ~+90% Ch. 95 duty → **~₹8,000 landed** (≈ 2× US).
- *Apple TV 4K* — $129 + $15 ship ≈ ₹12,000 CIF → ~+30% → **~₹15,000 landed** (vs ₹34,900 in India).

## 10. Best-value shortlist (top picks within ₹30k landed)

Ranked by **exclusivity × value**:

1. **Apple TV 4K** — ~₹15k landed vs ₹35k in India. Best pure arbitrage. (Electronics)
2. **Retroid Pocket 5 / Miyoo Mini Plus** — 2–3× cheaper than Indian resellers; ships here. (Electronics)
3. **Kobo Libra Colour / Clara Colour** — ~₹18–24k; *no India distributor at all*. (E-readers)
4. **Kindle Colorsoft** — ~₹21–26k; the only route to a color Kindle in India. (E-readers)
5. **Folio Society / B&N exclusive / signed books** — ₹3–7k, **duty-free**, often cheaper than local. (Books)
6. **Manga deluxe omnibus** (Berserk/Vagabond) — duty-free import beats grey-market figures on value. (Books)
7. **Pokémon Center exclusive plush + ETBs** — genuinely unavailable here; budget ~2× for duty. (Pokémon)
8. **Funko store exclusives / Ichiban Kuji figures** — true exclusives; light = cheaper to forward. (Collectibles/Anime)
9. **GMK keycap group buys** — permanently gone after the run; import or miss out. (Hobby)
10. **Premium Bandai Gunpla (HG/RG)** — far better selection/price than Indian resellers. (Hobby)
11. **Good Smile figma / S.H. Figuarts** — collector-grade articulation absent from Indian retail. (Anime)
12. **Lorier / Vaer microbrand watches** — Lorier *won't ship here*; needs a reshipper, but uniquely unavailable. (Watches)

**🛑 Skip importing:** Timex Q reissue, base G-Shock DW-5600, standard Anker chargers, common Nendoroids —
all available in India near or below import cost.

## Caveats

- Prices from **mid-2026** web research; FX ≈ ₹83/$ — all fluctuate (Prime Day, restocks, INR moves).
- **Landed ₹ are estimates.** Actual duty depends on declared value, courier assessment, and HSN
  classification on the day.
- **US-electronics warranty is not valid in India.** Verify 110V-only devices before buying.
- **Toys/figures/plush** carry real **BIS customs-detention risk** — factor it in.
