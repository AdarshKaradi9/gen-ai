Health optimizer

Your body is a sample size of one. Stop averaging it away.

Every tracker on the market answers what happened. None of them answer what to do about it, and none can prove that anything you changed actually worked. n=1 merges your wearables, labs and logs into a single record, estimates effects within your own data, and runs real randomized trials on you — one variable at a time.

Screen 01

One decision, not forty charts

The home screen commits to a single action for the day and shows the evidence behind it. The readiness number is demoted — it sits below the decision, banded by the user's own typical range so an ordinary 68 stops reading as a failing grade.

  • HierarchyAction first, score second, raw metrics last and deliberately dimmed.
  • DecomposeEvery point of the score is attributed to a logged input. No unexplained deltas.
  • HonestyConfidence and sample size ride alongside the claim, never hidden behind a tap.
  • RestraintMetrics with nothing to say are collapsed into one muted line.

Screen 02

The causal ledger

A forest plot, borrowed intact from clinical literature, because it is the right instrument: point estimate, interval, sample size, all legible at a glance. The zero line is the whole argument — anything whose whisker crosses it has not earned a behavior change yet.

  • RankingSorted by effect magnitude, so the biggest lever is always the top row.
  • Null resultFindings of "no effect" are shown, greyed, not deleted. Ruling things out is the product.
  • ColorBrick and moss encode direction; the petrol accent is reserved for interface, never for data.
  • SwappableThe outcome chip switches the whole ledger to energy, resting HR, glucose or mood.

Screen 03

Randomized trials, run on you

The feature no consumer tracker ships. The app randomizes the order of active and placebo nights, blinds the capsules, and holds the rest of your protocol fixed for the duration. The night grid is both a schedule and the study design, readable in one look.

  • DisciplineInterim results are shown but explicitly marked unactionable. No early calls.
  • Cost framingAdherence is motivated by what quitting costs — the answer — not by a streak.
  • IsolationConcurrent trials that would confound each other are blocked, with the reason given.
  • QueueNext trials are proposed from the ledger's widest intervals: test what you least know.

Screen 04

Blood work, finally on the same timeline

Four discrete lab draws overlaid on continuous wearable data. The toggle is the argument: a population reference range says you are fine, while your own two-year baseline says something changed. Both are shown; the personal one is the default.

  • Sparse vs. denseLab points stay discrete and marked; continuous data is dashed and recessive.
  • LinkageThe chart pairs a lab marker with the wearable signal that historically tracks it.
  • Next stepThe panel ends in an action — a dated draw with the exact markers to request.
  • SemanticsBrick, ochre and moss carry status; no traffic-light green is used decoratively.

Screen 05

Life is a covariate, not a failure

Illness, travel, altitude and cycle phase are the reason most people abandon trackers in month three: the numbers go red for reasons the app refuses to acknowledge. Here, detected context suspends the scoring and removes the day from the model rather than logging it as a loss.

  • ToneThe alert is statistical, not scolding — it states what changed and what was suspended.
  • StatisticsExcluding sick days is a modeling decision surfaced to the user, not hidden in a footnote.
  • ReversibleA single control returns you to normal scoring if the detection is wrong.
  • No debtNothing is owed on return. Missed targets vanish rather than accumulating.

Screen 06

Six partial copies of you, reconciled

Merging health sources is not a settings screen — it is where trust is won or lost. When two devices disagree about last night, n=1 says which one it kept, on what evidence, and lets you overrule it.

  • ArbitrationConflicts are surfaced with the reasoning, not silently resolved by priority order.
  • ProvenanceEvery number on every screen traces back to the source and device that produced it.
  • LifecycleEnded sources are archived, not deleted — their history still feeds past estimates.
  • ExitFull export and per-source deletion, stated plainly on the screen that collects the data.
