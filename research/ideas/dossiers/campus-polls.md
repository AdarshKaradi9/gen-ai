# Campus Polls (positive anonymous polls, TBH/Gas model)

## One-line pitch
Join your school, answer 12 positive polls a day about classmates ("best laugh", "most likely to start a company"), and get a flame notification every time someone picks you; pay to see hints about who voted.

## Who exactly
Two options. High school (13 to 18) is where the model has worked: Nikita Bier says invites per user fall about 20% for every year of age from 13 to 18 (self-reported, playbook). College (18 to 22) is the safer legal choice post-NGL but has lower invite density. Where they gather: school-specific Instagram and TikTok accounts, Snapchat group chats. No reliable audience sizes found beyond US enrollment, which I did not look up this round.

## Demand evidence
- TBH reached 40% of a school within 24 hours and peaked at about 360K installs a day (Nikita, self-reported, playbook).
- Gas hit 1M DAU within two months of an August 2022 launch, 3.1M downloads in November 2022, 7M lifetime, then only 2,900 downloads by September 2023 (TechCrunch citing data.ai, third-party). Discord bought it in January 2023 and shut it on 7 November 2023, mainly for the engineering talent (TechCrunch citing an internal memo).
- Clones are live in 2026: Valid - Compliment Classmates (released Dec 2025, 10K ratings, 4.79 stars, about 114K MAU, 1K new ratings a week) and Peek: Secret Compliment (Aug 2024, 9.5K ratings, about 44K MAU) (AppGoblin, third-party estimates).

## Money proof
- Gas monetised with "God Mode", a paid tier that reveals voter hints (press). I did not find a verified Gas revenue figure in this round.
- Valid: $10K+/month bucket, 100% IAP (AppGoblin, third-party floor).
- Peek: under $10K/month (AppGoblin, third-party).
- So the model monetises, but the current clones sit at roughly $10K/month, not Gas-scale.

## Competitor weaknesses
Valid reviews: a non-user received a text saying a classmate complimented her, and objected to being named in polls she never agreed to and to contact-list harvesting ("major breach of privacy"). Peek was the subject of an Arkansas news warning after students received "compliment" texts, and its open comments let people outside the school reply to students, with no age checks (local news). Critics said Gas "monetizes their anxiety" (CBC, expert view).

## The wedge
Consent-first and college-only: .edu email verification, you appear in polls only after you join (no contact-scraped names), poll-only (no chat, no comments, no custom questions), and the paid tier gives fun stats (your top traits, a monthly "aura" card) instead of anxiety-driven reveals. Launch one campus at a time with a campus Instagram account, as TBH did.

## The 2-second clip
Phone buzzes, lock screen: "Someone in Junior year picked you for 'most likely to make everyone feel welcome'". Reaction face. This is literally the Gas format and it already proved itself.

## Core loop sketch
- Onboarding aha: pick school, add 5 friends, answer first poll round; within minutes your first incoming vote lands.
- Daily action: poll rounds unlock every hour or few hours (Gas-style cooldown).
- Streak/reward: daily poll streak, flames received counter, school leaderboard (risky: popularity rankings).
- Shareable artifact: compliment card for Instagram stories ("I got picked for best smile 14 times this week").
- Paywall: subscription for hints and stats, about $6.99/week (Gas-style), placed on the inbox, not the onboarding.

## Risks
- Regulatory: FTC and LA DA's 2024 NGL settlement banned NGL from serving under-18s and cost $5M over fake messages and "reveal the sender" upsells. A paid "who voted" reveal for teens is now directly in the line of fire. COPPA and state teen social media laws add more.
- App Store: Guideline 1.2 says apps used primarily for "random or anonymous chat", "hot-or-not" voting or bullying "do not belong on the App Store"; 1.1.6 rejects anonymous SMS. Contact-based invites must be user-initiated, not "select all" (5.1.2).
- Retention: Gas collapsed from 3.1M downloads a month to 2,900 in under a year; Nikita calls durable social retention a once-a-decade black swan.
- Saturation: Valid and Peek already occupy the niche; growth needs synchronous school-by-school launches, which are operationally heavy for a solo founder.

| **Total** | **22/30** | |

## Verdict
Maybe, high variance. The mechanics are proven and the 2026 clone Valid shows there is still pull (about 114K MAU in under a year), but the money that made Gas work (paid reveals to teens) is exactly what the FTC punished at NGL, and the decay curve is brutal. Only worth it as a college-only, consent-first experiment run fast and cheap, treated as a hit-or-miss bet rather than a business.
