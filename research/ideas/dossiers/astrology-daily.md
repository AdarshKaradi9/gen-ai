# Astrology Daily: AI horoscopes built around your friend group

## One-line pitch
A daily AI astrology reading that is about you and the five people in your group chat: each morning you get your own card plus one "who to text, who to avoid" line about a named friend, and a compatibility card for any pair in the group.

## Who exactly
Beachhead: US women aged 18 to 30 who already know their big three (sun, moon, rising) and use astrology as social language. Survey evidence on how many believe is mixed: a January 2024 Harris Poll for Cosmopolitan found 62% of Gen Z at least somewhat believe, versus 83% of millennials, and Harris said it had expected Gen Z to lead because of TikTok (survey, credible). An EduBirdie survey updated May 2026 claims about 70% of Gen Z believe (commercial survey, methodology unverified). So the core buyer may be millennial women as much as Gen Z. Where they gather: AstroTok, r/astrology and group chats. I did not find verified TikTok hashtag counts or subreddit sizes within budget.

## Demand evidence
- Co-Star: Sensor Tower data cited in the July 2026 coverage of its sale puts it at about 4.3M monthly active users (third-party estimate via press). Midjourney bought Co-Star (announced 24 July 2026, Bloomberg), terms undisclosed, and founder Banu Guler became Midjourney's chief design officer. An acquired app whose team has moved on can stall, which leaves room.
- Co-Star Android still adds about 14K installs a week, 8.6M lifetime (AppGoblin estimate, Nov 2026).
- Screensdesign's astrology category: average app $124.8K/month revenue and 54.9K installs/month; Western astrology apps average $135.5K/month (third-party estimates).

## Money proof
- Nebula (OBRIO, Ukraine): about $500K to $550K/month (Screensdesign estimate); $516K/month (Surfshark citing AppMagic, May 2025); top-grossing US horoscope app in July 2024 (Statista citing AppMagic). All third-party estimates. Its model has three parts: a subscription after a 26-step quiz, a one-time birth-chart upsell shown straight after purchase, and per-minute psychic chat.
- Co-Star: under $10K/month on Android (AppGoblin, 100% IAP). The app is mostly free, with paid features, and iOS revenue is unknown. A large audience does not mean much money here.
- Small AI astrology: "Project A", an AI fortune-telling app, shows $64K MRR and $80K 30-day revenue on TrustMRR (provider-verified, June 2026), though its claim of 50M downloads is self-reported.
- Historical: the top 10 US astrology apps grossed $40M in 2019, up 65% (Sensor Tower via TechCrunch, 2020).
- I found no revenue figures for CHANI, The Pattern or Sanctuary.

## Competitor weaknesses
I did not pull fresh 1 to 2 star reviews. From analyst write-ups: Screensdesign calls Nebula's dashboard overwhelming and its 26-step onboarding a likely point of drop-off. Wikipedia's Nebula article says the service "most likely uses scam tactics" (contested), so the psychic-chat upsell model carries a trust cost. Co-Star readings are generic to everyone except you, and it monetises its social graph only weakly, as its revenue shows. Co-Star's future after the Midjourney deal is unclear.

## The wedge
Make the friend group the product. Co-Star has friend charts, but the daily reading is still solo. Here the daily card names a friend ("Maya's Mars is in your 7th today, let her plan the night"), and a group "cosmic weather" card ranks who is having the best day. AI writes in a funny, specific voice without the fear-based psychic upsells. Invites become core: the reading gets better with every friend who adds their birth time.

## The 2-second clip
A group-chat screenshot sits next to a card reading "Today: text Jess, avoid Priya, Leo moon energy is unhinged". Then the camera swipes to a pair compatibility card showing a 92% score.

## Core loop sketch
- Onboarding aha: birthdate, time and place, then your big three revealed with a sharp one-liner in about 20 seconds. Next, "add your group" via a link.
- Daily action: open the card, react to a friend's line, check the group ranking.
- Streak: days opened, with two freezes. A "Mercury retrograde survival" streak as a seasonal event.
- Shareable: compatibility cards and the daily group ranking, both watermarked.
- Paywall: last onboarding screen, $39.99/yr with a 7-day trial or $6.99/week. The free tier keeps the daily card and one friend. Unlimited friends, deep pair reports and romantic compatibility are paid. A one-time $9.99 pair report as a second offer.

## Risks
Saturation: dozens of apps, and Nebula outspends on ads. Co-Star's free tier anchors expectations at zero. Retention depends on friends joining; a solo user churns. Reading friends' birth data raises privacy questions (Surfshark has flagged astrology-app privacy). Midjourney may relaunch Co-Star with heavy AI investment. No medical risk.

## Verdict
Maybe, leaning build. The money is proven, but it sits with aggressive, upsell-heavy apps like Nebula, not with social, friend-based apps like Co-Star. The friend-group angle only works if the paywall sells depth (pair reports, unlimited friends) without breaking the invite loop. Co-Star's acquisition opens a window for a product with a fresher voice.
