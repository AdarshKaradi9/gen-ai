# GLP-1 Off-Ramp: a companion for keeping the weight off when the shots stop

## One-line pitch
The app for the half of GLP-1 users who stop within a year or two: a guided taper-and-maintenance plan that tracks weight against a "keep-it-off band", protects muscle with protein and two short strength sessions a week, and logs the return of food noise so users act before the regain starts.

## Who exactly
- Beachhead: US adults who are tapering or have stopped Wegovy/Zepbound/compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide, mostly for cost reasons, plus users planning to stop in the next 3 months.
- Size: KFF (Nov 2025 poll) found about 12% of US adults currently taking a GLP-1, and 56% of users say it is hard to afford; cost is among the most cited reasons for stopping (survey, verified via KFF release). Gallup reports 11% taking one for weight loss in 2026 (survey). Discontinuation estimates range from about 30% within one year (US cohort of 289,809, clinical summary, not verified at source) to about half within one year (press). Either way the "stopped" pool grows by millions per year.
- Where they gather: r/Ozempic, r/Zepbound, r/Semaglutide, r/tirzepatide (sizes not verified in this pass), Facebook GLP-1 groups, and TikTok "coming off Ozempic" / "Ozempic rebound" content.

## Demand evidence
- Regain fear is now front-page news. A Cambridge meta-analysis (eClinicalMedicine, March 2026, 6 RCTs, 3,200+ people) found 60% of lost weight regained within 52 weeks of stopping, modelled to plateau near 75% (peer-reviewed, via press). A Cleveland Clinic real-world study of 7,938 patients found milder regain (0.5% of body weight at one year, but 55% gained) (press release). Both studies drew wide coverage, which keeps the topic in feeds.
- Incumbents and big brands are writing content into this gap: WeightWatchers has "tapering off GLP-1" and "last 30 days on GLP-1" guides, MeAgain blogs "Can you stop GLP-1 without regaining it?", Noom sells a "Taper Off Guarantee". Content demand is high; a focused product is missing.
- Early clinical signal: a 2026 randomized pilot (39 adults) found app-based support or medically tailored meals, started within 30 days of stopping, slowed early regain (small study, press).
- Muscle concern: a Veru survey of 1,000 GLP-1 users (May 2026) found only 15% reported actual muscle loss, but muscle preservation is heavily marketed (vendor survey). Exploding Topics shows "GLP-1 supplement" searches grew from 5,920 to 74,000/month (Feb 2024 to Jan 2026, third-party estimate), evidence that users spend on GLP-1 adjacent products.

## Money proof
- trends.vc "GLP-1 Companion Apps" (Aug 2026, third-party estimates): 20 paying players, typical revenue $81K/yr. Shotsy $4.7M, MeAgain $1.9M, Glowise $535K, DreamMe $281K, GlucoPal $129K, Gala $81K.
- Shotsy: about 350K MAU, roughly a third premium, $49.99/yr (self-reported on a podcast); runs at least five concurrent price points from $9.99 to $19.99/month (LynkDose comparison, Sept 2026); ~31,000 App Store ratings. Earlier press: $1M run rate and $2M raise (Feb 2025, press).
- Noom bundles a taper guarantee with medication (company claim), proving that the "after" phase is a sellable promise.
- No evidence found of any app with revenue built specifically on the off-ramp phase. That is both the gap and the uncertainty.

## Competitor weaknesses
- Shotsy and MeAgain are built around the dose: shot logs, medication level curves, injection sites. When the user stops, the core loop (log the shot) disappears, so the app loses its reason to open. Shotsy complaints found in this pass were thin: mandatory account registration, no wearable integration, dated navigation (marlvel.ai, AI-generated summary, low confidence). Overall Shotsy sentiment is favourable (Kimola).
- MeAgain already covers protein and fiber logging by photo and barcode, so "protein tracker for GLP-1" alone is not a wedge.
- Noom and WeightWatchers tie maintenance to their own prescriptions or memberships at much higher prices.
- I could not retrieve 1-2 star review text directly from the App Store in this pass; review-theme evidence is weak.

## The wedge
Own the day the dose log goes quiet. Import the user's Shotsy/MeAgain history (or a quick manual entry of start weight, lowest weight, last dose), then run a 12-week taper and 12-month maintenance program with three daily units only: morning weight vs band, protein hit (photo), and a 2-minute "food noise" check (0-10). Strength is two 20-minute home sessions per week. The promise is concrete and measurable ("stay inside your band"), which on-drug apps do not offer.

## The 2-second clip
A phone screen showing a weight chart: a steep GLP-1 drop, then a shaded green "keep-it-off band" extending forward, with the user's dots staying inside it, overlaid text "Month 6 off Zepbound. Still in the band." Second variant: a food-noise dial creeping from 2 to 6 with an alert "Food noise is back. Here is today's plan."

## Core loop sketch
- Onboarding aha: 5 questions (drug, highest, lowest weight, last dose date, reason for stopping), then the personal band and a regain-risk forecast drawn from the published 60% figure versus "with plan". One real action: log today's weight.
- Daily: weigh-in, protein photo, food-noise slider; 60 seconds total.
- Streak: "days in band" as the central measure, with two freezes per month; weekly strength check-in.
- Shareable artifact: "Days kept off" card (e.g., "180 days off Wegovy, 31 lb still gone"), watermarked.
- Paywall: last onboarding screen after the forecast. $49.99/yr preselected (matches Shotsy anchor), $9.99/mo alternative, 7-day trial with explicit charge date. Optional "taper starter" for current users.

## Risks
- Medical: tapering advice must stay "discuss with your prescriber"; no dosing instructions. Low risk if framed as tracking and habits.
- Willingness to pay: many stop because of cost, so price sensitivity is high. This is the biggest unknown.
- Incumbent response: Shotsy or MeAgain can add a maintenance mode quickly and already own the users; MeAgain already writes about stopping.
- Retention: once weight is stable, motivation fades; the band must keep producing small wins.
- Saturation: 20 paying GLP-1 apps and many more free ones; ASO terms are crowded.

## Verdict
Maybe, leaning build as a focused wedge. The money is proven in GLP-1 companions and the off-ramp is a large, growing, under-served moment backed by fresh regain studies, but willingness to pay among cost-driven quitters is unproven and incumbents can copy. Validate with a landing page and "coming off GLP-1" TikToks before building.
