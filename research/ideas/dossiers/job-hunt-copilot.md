# Job Hunt Copilot

## One-line pitch
Paste or share a job posting, get a resume and cover letter tailored to it in seconds, and let the app track every application and nudge you to follow up, with a live "application funnel" you can screenshot.

## Who exactly
Beachhead: early-career white-collar job seekers (new grads, laid-off tech and marketing workers, career switchers) who are sending dozens to hundreds of applications. They gather in r/jobs, r/recruitinghell, r/cscareerquestions and r/resumes (I did not get fresh member counts in this pass), on LinkedIn, and in "job search update" content on TikTok. Rezi alone says it gets about 1M new users per year (self-reported on its TrustMRR profile), and Teal says 4M to 5M people have used its platform (company-reported, figures vary by source).

## Demand evidence
- Rezi: "about 1M new users every year" (self-reported, TrustMRR profile, Nov 2026).
- Teal: 4M users per one source and "crossed five million" per a Teal job listing; 100K+ Teal+ subscribers per one secondary source (company-reported via press, inconsistent).
- Teal acquired Ramped in December 2025 to add automated applications (Business Wire), which shows incumbents are moving toward auto-apply.
- Survey and search-trend data on application volume: not found in this pass.

## Money proof
- Rezi: 9,992 active subscriptions, ranked #13 on TrustMRR, revenue verified with Stripe. Search snippets show about $154K MRR and $207K 30-day revenue, $5.5M total (verified via Stripe on TrustMRR; the page fetch confirmed subscriptions but not the MRR line, so treat the dollar figure as likely but unconfirmed). Pricing $29/mo or $149 lifetime.
- A Spanish-language "Currículo rápido IA" listing showed $273K MRR in a search snippet, but TrustMRR pages in this category showed mixed-up boilerplate numbers, so I am not relying on it. ResumeHero's apparent $159K MRR was boilerplate; its page shows no active subscriptions.
- Teal: $4.2M revenue in 2024, not yet profitable (CB Insights and press, third-party). Raised $7.5M in 2024.
- Jobscan Premium: $49.95/mo or $89.95/quarter (review site, listing price).
Conclusion: people clearly pay, and pay a lot per month, while job hunting.

## Competitor weaknesses
- LazyApply: Trustpilot around 1.9 to 2.1 of 5, with about half of reviews at 1 star. Complaints: irrelevant applications, wrong form fills, automations silently stopping, hard to get refunds (review roundups citing Trustpilot).
- Huntr: well rated (4.5 Trustpilot on about 20 reviews, 4.8 Chrome store) but no live support, tailoring works best only for resumes built inside Huntr, autofill hit-or-miss.
- Teal: autofill still requires the user to review and submit; Simplify is described as a cheaper auto-apply rival.
- General pattern: desktop Chrome extensions; mobile is an afterthought. Spray-and-pray auto-apply is distrusted by users and recruiters.

## The wedge
Mobile-first and "share to app": in the LinkedIn or Indeed app, tap Share, and get a tailored one-page resume, a cover letter and a keyword match score before you leave the bus. Pair it with honest tracking: a follow-up queue ("message the recruiter today"), and a weekly funnel chart (applied, replied, interviewed, offered). Position against auto-apply: "fewer, better applications."

## The 2-second clip
Split screen: a job post on the left, a resume on the right where highlighted bullets rewrite themselves and a match score jumps from 41% to 87%.

## Core loop sketch
- Onboarding aha: upload resume, paste one posting, see the rewritten resume and score in under 30 seconds.
- Daily action: add today's applications (via share sheet), clear the follow-up queue.
- Streak/reward: "applications this week" goal with a bend-not-break streak; celebrate the first reply and first interview.
- Shareable artifact: the job-search funnel (Sankey-style) card, a format job seekers already post on Reddit (observed, not sourced here), and an "I got the offer" card.
- Paywall: after the first tailored resume, hard paywall for unlimited tailoring; weekly $6.99 or monthly $19.99 fits a 1 to 3 month search better than annual.

## Risks
- Saturation: Rezi, Teal, Huntr, Jobscan, Simplify, Kickresume and dozens of AI wrappers; ChatGPT does tailoring for free.
- Retention is anti-correlated with success: users churn the day they get hired. LTV is capped at a few months.
- LinkedIn and job boards can block scraping; auto-apply risks account bans.
- Paid acquisition in "career" is likely contested by funded players (no CPI data found).

## Scores
| Criterion | 1-5 | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Two-second aha | 3 | Match score jump and highlighted rewrite read fast, but text-heavy |
| Visual short-video hook | 3 | "I applied to 300 jobs" storytime works; the product itself is a document |
| Money already swims | 5 | Rezi ~10K active subs (Stripe-verified), Teal $4.2M (2024, third-party) |
| Daily reason to return | 3 | Real daily work during a search, then zero after hiring |
| Shareable moment | 3 | Funnel chart and offer card; rejection counts are shareable but bittersweet |
| Unit economics | 3 | High willingness to pay per month, short lifetime, crowded paid channels |
| **Total** | **20/30** | |

## Verdict
Maybe, leaning skip for a solo founder. Money is proven and large, but the category is saturated, ChatGPT is a free substitute, and success ends the subscription. Only worth building with a sharp mobile share-sheet wedge and an organic TikTok channel already in hand.
