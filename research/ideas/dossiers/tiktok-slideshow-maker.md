# TikTok Slideshow Maker

## One-line pitch
Paste a link or upload product photos and get a week of TikTok/Instagram photo carousels (hook slide, value slides, CTA) that a brand, app or creator can post daily.

## Who exactly
Beachhead: indie app developers and small DTC brands who already know "slideshow marketing" is the cheap organic channel. They gather on X (build-in-public), Indie Hackers, r/SaaS and r/indiehackers, and TrustMRR itself. The niche is visible on TrustMRR: SlidesTok's listing shows 1,346 X followers, OpenReels 20.9K (both listings, Nov 2026). Secondary: faceless "theme page" creators (stoicism, discipline, self-love), which is exactly the template set SlideFarm ships.

## Demand evidence
- Our playbook: Flamme's founder reports question slideshows convert 0.2-0.5% of views to installs versus 0.05-0.2% for memes (self-reported), and Playkit says plain hook-and-demo is fading in favour of slideshows and series.
- CineRads cites a 698,000-post analysis claiming TikTok carousels drove 81% higher engagement than comparable video (vendor-cited, not checked).
- A crowd of new entrants in 2026 (SlidesTok, SlideFarm, NeuroReel founded Jan 2026, ReelMoney, CineRads, Volume, Pippit by CapCut) is itself a demand signal and a saturation warning.

## Money proof
- ReelFarm (solo founder Matt Welter, launched 2024): "5,000+ paying customers" per a third-party review (unverified); $10K MRR claimed in a March 2025 SaaS Gallery post (self-reported). No verified 2026 MRR found. Pricing $19 / $49 / $95 / $195 per month (superscale.ai review).
- TrustMRR search snippets (payment-API verified, snapshots conflict across dates and pages, treat as rough): SlidesTok shown at $66K MRR / $407K total in one snapshot, but its live page on 6 Nov 2026 reads "No active subscriptions"; SlideFarm $69K 30-day revenue in one snapshot, live page shows 3 active subscriptions; NeuroReel $66K 30-day revenue with a $2.5M asking price. These numbers are not reliable enough to anchor on.
- ReelMoney founder: competitors "close to 30-40k MRR" (self-reported, on a for-sale listing).
- Pricing band across the field: $9 to $79/month (SlidesTok, ReelMoney, OpenReels listings).

## Competitor weaknesses
- ReelFarm: direct TikTok publishing locked behind the $49 tier; TikTok-only; credits burn on bad generations; "somewhat overpriced and doesn't support YouTube or Instagram publishing" (SourceForge review quoted by superscale.ai).
- Auto-posting carries account risk: "TikTok is penalizing automatic posting to some degrees" (superscale.ai).
- Output sameness: AI stock photo plus bold text hook is now recognisable, and formats die as audiences learn them (playbook caveat).

## The wedge
Not another generator. The gap is a closed feedback loop: connect the account, read which hooks and first slides actually got views and saves, and generate the next batch from the winners, with performance shown per hook. Pair that with a narrow vertical where nobody is optimised (for example local businesses or real-estate agents) and with the user's own photos rather than AI stock, which reads as more native.

## The 2-second clip
Screen recording: a URL is pasted, and a grid of nine finished carousels fans out with hooks like "3 apps that replaced my therapist". Second beat: a TikTok analytics screen showing one of them at 200K views.

## Core loop sketch
- Onboarding aha: paste link, get three free carousels with no account (CineRads already does exactly this).
- Weekly job: Monday batch of 7-14 slideshows, daily post reminder or scheduled post.
- Reward: weekly "your best hook" report with views per slideshow.
- Shareable artifact: the slideshows themselves are public; a watermark is unacceptable to paying brands, so virality comes from "how I got 1M views" founder posts, not the product.
- Paywall: after the free three; $29/month or $19/month annual, publishing on all tiers.

## Risks
Extreme saturation, including ByteDance's own Pippit with a free tier. Platform dependence on TikTok's posting API and on the format staying fresh. B2B churn is high when a user's views do not move, and image-generation costs eat margin at low tiers.

## Scores
| Criterion | 1-5 | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Two-second aha | 5 | Link in, finished slides out, instantly visible |
| Visual short-video hook | 4 | Meta demos plus view-count proof; works on X more than TikTok |
| Money already swims | 4 | Many paying tools, ReelFarm "5,000+ customers" claim; few verified figures |
| Recurring weekly job | 5 | Posting daily is the whole job |
| Shareable moment | 2 | Output is shared, but not attributed to the tool |
| Unit economics | 3 | $19-49/mo, cheap content-led CAC, but high churn and generation costs |
| **Total** | **23/30** | |

## Verdict
Maybe, leaning skip. Demand is real and the format is the one our playbook favours, but at least ten near-identical tools launched in 2025-2026 and TrustMRR numbers for them are inconsistent. Only worth building with a performance-feedback loop or a vertical nobody serves.
