# Close Friends Daily (one photo or prompt a day, a few friends only)

## One-line pitch
Each day a shared prompt ("show your view right now", "what you're eating") goes to your 5 to 10 closest friends; answers land on a home-screen widget, and the group keeps a streak and gets a monthly recap.

## Who exactly
Beachhead: teens and college students in tight friend groups and long-distance friends or couples, the audience that made Locket and BeReal. Where they gather: TikTok and Instagram, school group chats. Locket's own description pitches "best friends and family" with a 20-friend cap. No community sizes found beyond app MAU.

## Demand evidence
- Locket Widget: 1.7M ratings, 4.69 stars, about 4.4M MAU and 3.4K new ratings a week as of Nov 2026 (AppGoblin, third-party estimate). An earlier indexed AppGoblin snapshot showed 1.6M MAU and $200K+/month, so estimates move a lot.
- BeReal: peaked around 73.5M monthly users in Aug 2022, then declined; Voodoo bought it for EUR 500M in 2024 and claims about 40M MAU (company-reported, inconsistent with its own 30M figure elsewhere); third parties put it near 16M MAU in March 2025 (press and third-party estimates).
- Airchat (2024 social audio) paused invites and was estimated in the low tens of thousands of users (Engadget); I found no 2025 status. Retro: no current data found.

## Money proof
- Locket: raised $12.5M (Sam Altman, Mike Krieger); Locket Gold subscription launched 2024 (ad removal, widget customisation, engagement stats) and grew to "many thousands of users"; subscription revenue rose 77% after paywall testing (Helium case study, vendor/self-reported). Ad revenue up 87% after a Moloco SDK switch (Moloco case study, vendor). AppGoblin: about $500K+/month, 36% IAP and 64% ads (third-party estimate).
- BeReal: EUR 500M acquisition price (press), introduced ads to user pushback.
- Read: money exists but is ad-heavy at massive scale; subscription is a minority of revenue even for the category leader.

## Competitor weaknesses
Locket reviewers want Gold perks free and ask for basic features like replies; the strongest reviews praise exactly what it is (no scrolling, 20 friends). BeReal decline coverage points to notification fatigue, "BeFake" posing and novelty loss, then ads. The space's weakness is not product quality, it is that Locket already owns the widget habit with 4M+ MAU.

## The wedge
Prompt-led rather than open camera: one shared daily question or photo theme per group, which gives a reason to post on boring days, plus a group streak (every member posts) and a printed-feel monthly zine. Angle options: long-distance friends after graduation, or families (grandparents get a widget of grandkids). Both are thin differentiators against Locket adding prompts in a sprint.

## The 2-second clip
Unlock phone: the home-screen widget flips to a friend's photo with the day's prompt "your view at 5pm" and a "group streak 41" badge. Then a split grid of five friends' answers.

## Core loop sketch
- Onboarding aha: requires at least one friend to join; solo users see nothing (the classic cold start).
- Daily action: answer the prompt with one photo.
- Streak/reward: group streak that breaks if anyone misses, with freezes.
- Shareable artifact: monthly recap video or grid, which Locket already does.
- Paywall: freemium only (social apps cannot hard-wall before network effects); $3.99/month for widget themes, larger groups, recap exports.

## Risks
- Saturation and platform: Locket (4M+ MAU), BeReal, Instagram Close Friends and iOS shared albums cover this; Apple widget rules can change.
- Retention: BeReal shows the daily-prompt format decays after novelty.
- Moderation: UGC photos between minors require Guideline 1.2 filtering, reporting, blocking.
- Economics: low ARPU, ad-reliant at scale, and invite density falls with age.

## Verdict
Skip. The format is proven, but Locket owns it with an estimated 4M+ MAU and a refined widget, and BeReal shows the daily-prompt mechanic decays. A solo founder would need a new network to form from scratch with ad-scale economics; a narrow family or long-distance angle is too small and too easy to copy.
