# Selfie Colour Analysis ("Find Your Season")

## One-line pitch
Take one selfie in daylight and get your seasonal colour type (for example "Soft Autumn"), a palette of the colours that make you glow versus wash you out, and a share card of your palette.

## Who exactly (the beachhead audience and where they gather online, with sizes)
Women aged 16-35 who follow beauty and style creators and have seen the "draping" videos, where a stylist holds coloured fabric under a face. The habit is shopping and makeup choice, so the second beachhead is people about to buy makeup, hair dye or a capsule wardrobe.
- TikTok #coloranalysis: over 600M to 655M views in undated articles (press, unverified, likely older than 2026).
- The trend was still covered as "the latest fashion trend to go viral" in March 2026 (Offaly Express).
- Subreddit sizes: not found in my searches.

## Demand evidence (searches, communities, trend signals, with sources)
- Mainstream explainers (ET Online, Dexerto, Yahoo) show a long-running trend; colour analysis is a decades-old cycle, which suggests durable interest.
- Dressika (Android) still adds about 8.3K installs a week and 45K over four weeks (AppGoblin, Oct 2026, third-party estimate).
- Many new entrants in 2025-2026 (Glamour, Palette, Hue IQ, Color Analysis AI: Season, Hue Are You), a sign of builder demand and of saturation.

## Money proof (competitors with revenue/downloads, pricing, labelled by evidence type)
- Glamour (AI selfie colour analysis): about $23K MRR and $24K revenue in the last 30 days, 254 active subscriptions, about 9% churn, grown from $222 to $15.8K MRR in 10 months on Meta ads in Turkey alone (TrustMRR, RevenueCat-verified, Nov 6 2026). Only 77 App Store ratings, so revenue comes from paid acquisition, not virality.
- Color Analysis AI: 70K downloads, $40K MRR and 58M TikTok views in about five months using 20+ TikTok accounts; WhatColors reportedly $60K MRR in six months (Shortimize blog, self-reported/marketing, unverified).
- Dressika: about 6.7M Android installs, about 238K MAU, around $10K+/month, 54% IAP and 46% ads (AppGoblin, third-party estimate). Another tracker shows about $70K lifetime revenue on iOS (The App Launchpad, third-party estimate). Large audience, weak monetisation.

## Competitor weaknesses (what 1-2 star reviews and complaints say)
- Inconsistency: "tried the same photo several different times, each attempt returned another analysis" (My Best Colors review, Justuse aggregator). Palette reviewers report different wrong results per photo.
- Lighting: a colour consultancy tested three popular AI apps and found only about a 50% chance of getting the right broad season (Kettlewell Colours, vendor with a bias against apps, but the most systematic test found).
- Skin tone misreads: a Dressika reviewer said the app read their skin as much darker than reality.

## The wedge (the specific angle that beats incumbents)
Trust and consistency. Guide the user through a 20-second capture (face a window, white paper beside the face as a reference, no makeup), reject bad lighting with a confidence score, and show a live "drape" comparison: the same selfie with a flattering colour and an unflattering colour side by side. That turns the result into something the user can see, not just a label. Second wedge: after the reveal, a "will this suit me?" scanner for any product photo or shop link, which creates a reason to come back while shopping.

## The 2-second clip
Split screen of the same face: left under a mustard drape looking tired and grey, right under a dusty rose drape looking bright, with the caption "I am a Soft Summer" and the palette card sliding up.

## Core loop sketch
- Onboarding aha: guided selfie, then an animated drape test reveal before any account.
- Daily/weekly action: scan clothes or makeup while shopping ("suits you 92%"), and a weekly "outfit in your palette" idea. Honestly, most users need the result once.
- Streak/reward: weak; best is a collection of "approved" items and a wardrobe colour score.
- Shareable artifact: palette card with season name, top colours and "avoid" colours, watermarked. Friends compare seasons, which drives installs.
- Paywall: hard paywall right before the full reveal (season name free, palette and drapes locked). Weekly $4.99-6.99 or annual $29.99-39.99, mirroring the Quittr-style hard paywall that suits one-time reveals.

## Risks (regulatory, platform, saturation, retention)
- Retention is the core weakness: the answer does not change, so churn after the reveal is high; revenue depends on the first purchase.
- Saturation: dozens of lookalikes; CPI on Meta rises as they all bid on the same audience.
- Accuracy disputes harm reviews; skin tone bias across ethnicities is a reputational risk.
- Biometric photo handling (face data) needs a clear privacy policy, especially in the EU and Illinois.

## Verdict
Maybe, leaning build as a fast, cheap launch rather than a long-term habit business. Money is proven at small scale (Glamour verified at $23K MRR on paid ads in one country), and the content format is native to TikTok. Expect a hit-driven, high-churn revenue curve unless a shopping scanner gives a real reason to return.
