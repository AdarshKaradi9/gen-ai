# Baby Sleep and Feed Predictor

## One-line pitch
A one-tap baby tracker for both parents whose home screen answers the only question that matters at 6am: "When is the next nap?"

## Who exactly
- **Beachhead:** first-time parents of babies 0-18 months in the US, UK and Australia. The US recorded 3,606,400 births in 2025, down 1% (CDC NCHS provisional data, verified government statistic).
- **Where they gather:** r/NewParents, r/sleeptrain, r/beyondthebump, baby sleep consultant Instagram and TikTok accounts (subscriber counts not retrieved). The audience refreshes every year, which is good for acquisition and bad for lifetime.

## Demand evidence
- Huckleberry has 121K iOS ratings at 4.92 stars and is still collecting about 414 new ratings a week; AppGoblin estimates about 322K iOS MAU (third-party estimate). It claims 5+ million families (self-reported).
- Sensor Tower data shows Huckleberry US revenue peaking around $69K in a single week in January 2025 and up to 25.9K weekly downloads (Sensor Tower blog, third-party estimate).
- Baby sleep consulting is a paid profession, and Huckleberry itself sells a $49.99 Sleep Improvement Plan (Adapty paywall library).

## Money proof
- **Huckleberry:** estimates vary widely: about $400K/month and 100K monthly downloads (Adapty text), $750K-$800K/month (screensdesign, Adapty header), versus "$10K+" (AppGoblin floor bucket). All third-party estimates. Sensor Tower's ~$69K peak US week implies roughly $250K-$300K per US month, which supports the middle of the range. Pricing: Plus $9.99-$11.99/month or $58.99/year, Premium $14.99/month or $119.99/year, 14-day trial, soft paywall after the onboarding quiz (Adapty, screensdesign).
- **Napper, Baby Tracker (Nighp), Sprout, Glow Baby, Baby Daybook:** established competitors; I found no revenue figures for Napper in this pass.

## Competitor weaknesses
A 2026 analysis of 1-2 star reviews across the top five trackers (unstar.app) found five recurring complaints:
1. The feature parents downloaded for (sleep prediction, charts, history) is behind a subscription. Huckleberry's is "sleep features paywalled, Premium price"; one App Store reviewer complained SweetSpot moved behind a paywall and the sleep questionnaire needs Premium, not Plus.
2. Sync between two caregivers fails.
3. Data lost after updates or phone changes.
4. Logging is slow when you need it fast at 3am.
5. Reminders misfire.
Screensdesign also notes Huckleberry's post-onboarding questionnaire is very long and SweetSpot needs two days of data before it shows value.

## The wedge
Free, rock-solid two-parent sync and a free basic nap window (the thing competitors lock), with the paid tier on coaching: an AI sleep plan that adapts daily and explains why the window moved. One-handed logging from the lock screen and Live Activity countdown. A second angle is non-English markets (Spanish, Hindi, Portuguese), where Huckleberry is less localized.

## The 2-second clip
A phone on a nursery dresser shows a Live Activity: "Next nap in 0:12, sweet spot 10:40-10:55", with a soft countdown ring turning from green to amber. Caption: "I stopped guessing."

## Core loop sketch
- **Onboarding aha:** enter age and last wake time; first nap window appears immediately (no two-day wait).
- **Daily action:** 6-12 one-tap logs a day; feeds, diapers, sleep.
- **Streak/reward:** weekly "sleep got better" report; milestone cards ("first 3-hour stretch").
- **Shareable artifact:** a weekly sleep chart and "longest night yet" card that parents send to grandparents or post.
- **Paywall:** soft paywall after the first predicted window; $8.99/month or $49.99/year, annual preselected, 7-day trial. Undercut Huckleberry Premium ($119.99).

## Risks
- **Saturation:** Huckleberry is excellent (4.9 stars, Editors' Choice) and owns the "sweet spot" mental model; free trackers are plentiful.
- **Short lifetime:** need fades after 12-24 months, so LTV is capped; parenting CPI is high.
- **Medical/safe sleep:** must avoid medical claims and follow safe-sleep guidance; Huckleberry lists in the Medical category.
- **Data trust:** one data-loss incident kills reviews.

## Verdict
Maybe. The money is clearly there and complaints about paywalled predictions and broken sync are consistent, but the incumbent is beloved and the category is crowded. Build only with a sharp wedge (free prediction plus flawless sync, or a localized market) and organic parent-creator distribution.
