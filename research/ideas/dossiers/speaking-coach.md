# Speaking Coach: a daily 2-minute speaking rep

## One-line pitch
Every day you answer one prompt out loud for 60 to 120 seconds (an interview question, a "tell me about yourself", a toast), and AI scores filler words, pace, clarity and confidence, then shows your score climbing over weeks.

## Who exactly
The evidence says the money sits with two groups, not with "people afraid of public speaking" in general:
1. Non-native English speakers preparing for interviews and work (the ELSA and Speak audience). ELSA has about 43.6M Android installs (AppGoblin) and claims 50M users (self-reported).
2. Job seekers practising interviews, the audience Yoodli started with before moving to enterprise.
Beachhead: non-native English-speaking professionals and students in India, Southeast Asia and the US who have job interviews in English within 30 days. They gather in r/cscareerquestions, r/jobs, LinkedIn, and English-learning TikTok. I did not verify community sizes or hashtag counts in this budget.

## Demand evidence
- ELSA Speak: about 41K Android installs a week, 272K over four weeks, "steady growth" (AppGoblin estimate, Oct 2026).
- Speak (AI language tutor, adjacent): passed $100M annualised revenue (Forbes, via Benzinga), $1B valuation in Dec 2024 (press). Strong proof that people pay to practise speaking with AI.
- Yoodli: average recurring revenue up 900% in a year (press, self-reported, no dollar figure).
- Standalone consumer speaking apps show weak demand: Speeko has 6.6K ratings and about 8K MAU (AppGoblin); Orai claims 300K users lifetime (self-reported on Product Hunt).

## Money proof
- Yoodli: $13.7M Series A (May 2025), $40M Series B led by WestBridge (Dec 2025), about $60M total (press; CB Insights). "Majority of revenue now comes from enterprise clients" (press). Signal: consumer was not where Yoodli's money ended up.
- ELSA: 2023 run rate $32.5M (Latka estimate) to $49.6M (Datanyze estimate), conflicting; $23M Series C 2023 (press). AppGoblin shows only $10K+ a month on Android (lower-bound bucket; ELSA sells heavily via web and Asia, so treat as unreliable).
- Speak: $100M+ annualised revenue (Forbes via press, company-confirmed).
- Speeko: under $10K a month, 100% IAP, 4.7 stars (AppGoblin estimate). Endorsed by voice coach Roger Love, yet still small.
- Orai and Poised: no revenue data found. Poised pivoted to meeting-assistant real-time feedback (Product Hunt).

## Competitor weaknesses
Review data was thin in this budget. Poised reviewers criticise flexibility and ease of use and show no recent reviews (Product Hunt), suggesting a stalled product. Orai reviews are mostly old (2019-era Capterra). Speeko is a lesson library with limited daily pull. Yoodli is web-first and has shifted to enterprise roleplay, leaving the mobile consumer daily habit open. ELSA focuses on word-level pronunciation drills rather than whole-answer confidence for a real interview. None I found frames speaking as a daily 2-minute rep with a visible progress score.

## The wedge
Interview-day outcome plus daily reps. Onboarding asks for your interview date and role, then builds a countdown plan of daily 2-minute answers to likely questions for that role, scored on filler words and structure (STAR), with accent clarity scoring for non-native speakers. Incumbents are either pronunciation drills (ELSA) or enterprise roleplay (Yoodli). The countdown creates urgency that justifies a weekly plan price.

## The 2-second clip
Before and after audio waveforms side by side: Day 1 with "um" highlighted in red 14 times, Day 14 with 2, and a confidence score jumping from 48 to 81.

## Core loop sketch
- Onboarding aha: answer "Tell me about yourself" once in onboarding; in 5 seconds see your filler count, words per minute and one specific fix, then re-record and see the score improve on the spot.
- Daily action: one 2-minute prompt tailored to the target role.
- Streak: daily rep streak with two freezes, framed as "reps until interview day".
- Shareable: "Um count: 14 to 2" card and a short before/after audio clip.
- Paywall: right after the first score, $7.99/week for interview sprints and $49.99/yr preselected for ongoing practice, 3-day trial.

## Risks
Saturation from horizontal AI: ChatGPT voice mode and Gemini can already roleplay interviews for free, which is the main threat. Retention: interview prep is episodic; users churn after landing the job, so LTV depends on weekly pricing during a sprint. Accent scoring is sensitive and could read as bias; frame as clarity, not "accent reduction". Inference and speech-model costs per rep. No medical risk. Microphone permission must be earned in onboarding.

## Verdict
Skip as a general confidence app; maybe as a narrow interview-sprint tool for non-native English speakers. The money evidence is in language learning (Speak, ELSA) and enterprise (Yoodli), while consumer speaking-confidence apps like Speeko stayed small, and free AI voice modes erode the core feature.
