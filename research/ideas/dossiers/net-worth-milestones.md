# Net Worth Milestones

## One-line pitch
A FIRE tracker built around one number, your "freedom date," that counts down as you save, with milestone cards (first $10K, $100K, Coast FI, "25% free") designed to be shared without revealing your balance.

## Who exactly
Beachhead: 25 to 40 year old salaried savers in tech, healthcare and engineering who follow FIRE. They gather on r/financialindependence (about 2.3M to 2.4M members, third-party trackers, 2025 to 2026), r/Fire (about 700K, GummySearch snapshot before its Nov 2025 closure), r/fatFIRE (about 488K, Hive Index), the ChooseFI and Mr Money Mustache audiences, and "net worth at 27" content on TikTok (view counts not found).

## Demand evidence
- r/financialindependence at roughly 2.4M members (Hive Index, third-party).
- ProjectionLab's founder credits a Mr Money Mustache shoutout and a ChooseFI feature as growth inflection points, which shows the FIRE community distributes tools (self-reported).
- Monarch grew about 20x after Mint shut down in Nov 2023, becoming the Reddit consensus replacement (Sacra, third-party).

## Money proof
- ProjectionLab (solo-founded FIRE planner, bootstrapped): $1M ARR, $83.3K MRR in June 2025, 100,000+ households (founder blog, self-reported). The founder's Indie Hackers post cites $52K+/mo around the same time, and Latka estimated about $17K/mo in 2023, so figures conflict by definition and date. Even so, it is the strongest solo-founder proof in this batch.
- Monarch: about $60M ARR end of 2025 and $100M ARR by Sept 2026, $75M Series B at $850M valuation in May 2025, $99/yr (Sacra, third-party estimate). 500K+ paying subscribers by March 2026 (Sacra).
- Copilot Money: profitable in 2023, 100,000+ subscribers, $6M Series A in March 2024 (company-reported).
- Kubera and Empower revenue: not found. Empower is free and monetizes via wealth management.

## Competitor weaknesses
I did not gather 1 to 2 star reviews in this pass. Known structural gaps from the evidence:
- Monarch and Copilot are budgeting-first; net worth is one tab and FIRE projections are shallow.
- ProjectionLab is powerful but a desktop-style planner with a learning curve; it is not a habit or a share engine.
- Bank aggregation costs about $0.30 per connection per month (Sacra), so incumbents need $99/yr pricing; sync breakage is a common complaint category for all aggregator apps (general knowledge, not sourced here).

## The wedge
Make the freedom date the hero, not the ledger. Manual or semi-manual entry (monthly balance snapshot in 60 seconds, optional sync) keeps costs low. Every contribution shows "this moved your freedom date 9 days earlier." Milestone cards share percentages and dates ("I'm 31% free," "Coast FI at 34"), not dollar amounts, which removes the privacy barrier to sharing.

## The 2-second clip
A big countdown reads "Freedom date: March 2041," the user logs a $1,500 deposit, and the date flips to "November 2040" with a confetti burst.

## Core loop sketch
- Onboarding aha: age, savings, monthly saving, spending; instantly show freedom date and "% free."
- Daily action: light, and deliberately weekly: log a no-spend day or a contribution and watch the date move; a monthly "net worth check-in" ritual.
- Streak/reward: weekly saving streak; milestone ladder (first $10K, $100K, Lean FI, Coast FI, Barista FI).
- Shareable artifact: milestone cards with % and date only; a yearly "freedom date moved X months" recap.
- Paywall: after the freedom-date reveal; scenarios, multiple goals and sync behind $59.99/yr or $7.99/mo, annual preselected.

## Risks
- Saturation at the top: Monarch, Copilot, Empower (free), Kubera, ProjectionLab, plus spreadsheets that FIRE users love.
- Daily frequency is low by nature; checking net worth daily during market swings can feel bad (wellbeing line).
- Projections may look like investment advice; keep it educational to avoid adviser rules.
- Aggregator dependence and costs (Plaid, MX) if sync is offered.
- Audience is spreadsheet-proud and skeptical of paying; ProjectionLab shows they will pay for depth, not for gloss.

## Verdict
Maybe, the best of these four. ProjectionLab proves a solo founder can reach seven figures selling to the FIRE community, and the share-without-dollars milestone card is a fresh distribution angle. The risk is that this audience values depth over gamification, so the freedom date must sit on top of credible projections, not replace them.
