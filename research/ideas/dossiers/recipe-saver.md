# Recipe Saver from Social Video

## One-line pitch
Paste or share any TikTok, Reel or YouTube Short and get a clean recipe card, a step-by-step cook mode and a merged grocery list, even when the recipe was only spoken aloud.

## Who exactly
- **Beachhead:** home cooks aged 20-40 who save food videos they never cook, in the US, Australia and the UK (ReciMe's top markets per Adapty).
- **Where they gather:** FoodTok and Instagram Reels food creators, r/MealPrepSunday, r/EatCheapAndHealthy, r/Cooking (subscriber counts not retrieved). The pain ("I have 400 saved recipe Reels") is itself a common content format.

## Demand evidence
- ReciMe has 401K iOS ratings at 4.77 stars and is adding about 5.7K new ratings a week; AppGoblin estimates about 1.6M iOS MAU (third-party estimate). It grew from 20,000 to over 400,000 users in 2023 (SmartCompany, self-reported) and raised a $1.5M seed.
- App Store reviewers single out social import: "I love how easy it is to import recipes from TikTok or pictures. Even though it doesn't always work perfectly, it's still a huge time saver."
- Many 2025-26 entrants (Honeydew, The Recipe Library, Pestle, Mela) pitch the same social import, which confirms demand and signals crowding.

## Money proof
- **ReciMe:** monthly revenue estimates span a wide range: $100K+ (AppGoblin, a floor bucket), about $100K with 200K monthly downloads (Adapty text), about $150K (screensdesign app page), and $1M with 750K monthly installs (screensdesign showcase). Annual estimates of about $2.1M-$2.2M (Prospeo, Datanyze). All third-party estimates. **Our notes' figure of $500K-$800K/month sits inside this range but I could not confirm it from any single source.** Pricing: annual plans from $34.99 to $119.99 and a $4.99 monthly option; trial with notification warm-up before the paywall and a 4.8-star social proof screen.
- **Honeydew:** about 51K iOS installs, ~19K MAU, under $10K/month (AppGoblin); Plus at $6.99/month or $39.99/year.
- **Paprika, Mela:** paid-upfront recipe managers with loyal followings; no revenue data found in this pass. Pestle: no data found.

## Competitor weaknesses
- Imports are inaccurate or altered for a noticeable share of users (third-party roundup puts it near 10%); some sites refuse to import (App Store reviews).
- ReciMe's own help centre admits Smart Import grabs only one recipe per post and suggests screenshot or paste workarounds.
- Rating declines are linked to aggressive subscription pushes; users ask for a lifetime option; one 2026 release reportedly applied free-tier import limits to paying users (automated market report, not confirmed).
- Paprika and Mela were built for websites, not video.

## The wedge
Reliability on the hardest inputs: videos where the recipe is only spoken (audio transcription plus on-screen text), multi-recipe posts, and creator captions in other languages. Pair that with a lifetime purchase option and a "cook this week" planner that turns a pile of saves into five dinners and one grocery list. A second angle is cuisine-specific (Indian, Korean) where measurements are loose and existing parsers fail.

## The 2-second clip
Screen recording: tap Share on a chaotic 40-second TikTok of someone making gochujang pasta with no written recipe, pick the app, and one second later a tidy card appears with 8 ingredients, quantities, 5 steps and a "creator: @..." credit. Caption: "It even caught the stuff she only said out loud."

## Core loop sketch
- **Onboarding aha:** onboarding asks the user to paste their most recent saved food video; the first card appears before signup.
- **Daily/weekly action:** share-sheet saves through the week; Sunday "plan my week" turns saves into a menu and grocery list.
- **Streak/reward:** "cooked it" check-offs and a monthly "you cooked 9 of your saves" card.
- **Shareable artifact:** a beautiful recipe card with creator credit, and the grocery list sent to a partner.
- **Paywall:** soft paywall after the third import; $5.99/month or $34.99/year, plus a $79 lifetime. Free tier capped at 10 imports a month.

## Risks
- **Platform:** scraping TikTok and Instagram content may breach their terms; platforms can break link parsing at any time. Creator attribution matters to avoid backlash.
- **Saturation:** ReciMe is large and well rated, and new entrants launch monthly; ASO for "recipe keeper" is contested.
- **Retention:** saving is frequent, cooking is not; many users save but never open the app again.
- **AI cost:** video and audio processing per import costs more than text parsing.

## Verdict
Maybe, close to build. Demand and money are the clearest in this batch and the clip is perfect, but ReciMe already owns the category, so a newcomer needs a visibly better import on spoken-only videos and a fairer pricing story. Build if a quick test shows your parser beats ReciMe on 20 hard TikToks.
