# Competitor teardown: screen-time apps (November 2026)

Evidence labels: **verified** (read directly from App Store listing or iTunes API on 6 Nov 2026), **3P est.** (third-party estimate: AppGoblin, ScreensDesign, Adapty), **self-reported** (founder or company claim), **review** (user review or vendor review roundup, indicative only). AppGoblin revenue buckets ("$10K+") are floors, not estimates, and clearly understate the big apps, so I quote ScreensDesign/Adapty for revenue and AppGoblin for ratings and MAU.

## Key takeaways for the build

- **Our exact combination does not exist yet, but every piece does.** No app I found ships a healing character plus friends notified on bypass plus a student mode. But Brainrot already owns the decaying pet and the "years of your life" onboarding screen, BePresent already pitches "25 years on your phone" plus friend leaderboards and group sessions, and ScreenZen added an accountability partner in September 2026. Our wedge is the combination and the audience, not any single feature.
- **"Years of your life" is not a differentiator.** Brainrot's onboarding shows the years you will lose (ScreensDesign teardown) and BePresent's listing opens with "25 years" (verified). Use it, but do not lead the pitch with it as if new.
- **Friend accountability is a fast-growing but small niche of tiny apps.** Friend Controls (1.1K ratings), Snitch, Narc, TimeBack, FriendLock and Unglue all launched 2025-2026, none above about 2.7K US ratings (verified). The one big app with real social features is BePresent (97K ratings, 1.1K new ratings per week, the fastest momentum in the category by that signal). Friends-as-feature is proven to be wanted; friends-as-whole-product has not broken out.
- **The pet that heals is open territory, narrowly.** Brainrot's brain decays and the user reviews say "my brain is healing," but its health bar is a punishment meter. Focus Friend (Hank Green) and Forest reward focus sessions, not daily screen time. A character that visibly recovers and bends rather than dies is still unclaimed as the core mechanic.
- **Student mode is thinly served.** Opal sells an "Opal Student Weekly" at $9.99 (verified) but no student-specific product; Focus Friend and Forest are student-coded focus timers, not blockers; dedicated "exam mode" apps (ScrollStop, Study G, TakeTime) are tiny. A finals-season product with campus groups is open.
- **Price anchors: annual $20 to $60 is the band, $99.99 draws anger.** one sec $19.99, Jomo $29.99, Clearspace $44.99, Brainrot $29.99 to $59.99, BePresent $24.99 to $59.99, Opal $99.99 (all verified). Subscription surprise is the top complaint (24% of 1 to 3 star reviews, review). A sub-$35 annual price with a charge reminder is a credible position.
- **Onboarding length: long works for this category.** Brainrot 28 steps, BePresent 32, Opal 24, all with emotional "shock" moments before the paywall; one sec (4) and Clearspace (7) are short with soft paywalls (3P). Expect 20 to 30 screens with a hard or near-hard paywall at the end.
- **The real threat is BePresent, not Opal.** It already combines friends, group sessions, money stakes, real-world rewards and the years framing, and is growing fast. If BePresent adds a character, our differentiation shrinks to audience and honest billing.

## App-by-app

**Opal** (iOS, Mac; Dec 2020). Scheduled and on-demand blocking via Screen Time API, Focus Score, "gems" milestones, friend leaderboard, guest-pass referral. Onboarding 24 steps; aha is a personalised focus report and before/after screen-time chart; soft paywall, 7-day trial on yearly (3P). Prices: $4.99 or $9.99/week, $19.99/month, $99.99/year, Opal Student Weekly $9.99, Pro $49.99 (verified). 203K ratings, 4.72, about 559K MAU (3P); about $400K to $600K/month and 200K downloads/month (Adapty, 3P); $4.8M 2024 revenue and about $6.9M raised (Lazyweb, 3P). Complaints: $99.99 renewal surprise, blocks vanish when the Screen Time permission is removed, silent failures after iOS updates (review).

**Brainrot** (iOS; May 2025, Smolworks, Yoni Smolyar and Brock Anderson). Cute brain pet whose health drops as you scroll; intervention walls, timed sessions (5 to 30 min), a fake FaceTime call "from your brain" when you open Instagram. Onboarding 28 steps with a decay slider, an "attention profile" label and a years-of-life visual; hard paywall with yearly and weekly, then a "50% off forever" pop-up (3P). Prices: $4.99/week, $8.99/month, yearly $19.99 to $59.99 in several tiers (verified). 43K ratings, 4.40, about 169K MAU (3P). Founder says about $400K/month, "a couple million downloads," about 150K users, launch from a 200K-follower creator audience (self-reported, Starter Story); ScreensDesign estimates $260K/month (3P). Complaints: bait pricing ($5/week then a discount on exit felt "scammy"), automations and widgets resetting, clunky UI, defaults to bricking the whole phone (review).

**one sec** (iOS, Android, Mac, browser; Sep 2020). A breathing pause or other intervention before a chosen app opens; Max Planck-linked research. Onboarding 4 steps, quiz recommends an intervention mode; soft paywall with 1-week trial (3P). Prices: $6.99/month, $19.99/year, lifetime $99.99, family plans $9.99/month, $29.99/year, $149 lifetime (verified). 104K ratings, 4.81, about 257K MAU (3P); revenue $80K to $150K/month (ScreensDesign, 3P, low confidence). Complaints: you tap through the pause on autopilot after a week; lifetime price feels high "for college students" (review).

**Brick** (iOS app plus $59 NFC puck; app Sep 2023; Wisconsin, TJ Driver and Zach Nasgowitz). Tap the puck to lock apps, leave it elsewhere; five emergency unbricks. New Brick Night ($129, Bluetooth bedroom blocker, Oct 2026, press release). App is free, hardware is the revenue. 69K ratings, 4.94, about 276K MAU (3P); "over 500,000 users in 180 countries" and "multiple eight figures" revenue (self-reported, Entrepreneur). Complaints: price of hardware, emergency-unbrick limits, deleting the app is the only escape (vendor FAQ, review).

**ScreenZen** (iOS, Android; Jul 2021). Free; delays that grow with each open, scroll interruptions, strict blocks. Version 4.0 (Sep 2026) added a "Minimal" mode that strips Reels and Shorts, Halo location modes, and "an accountability partner who can let you back in" (verified release notes). Tips only: $5 to $40 (verified). 109K ratings, 4.85, about 344K MAU, 545 new ratings per week (3P). Free, so it is the default roundup pick; no character.

**BePresent** (iOS; Sep 2022; New York, Jack and Charles Winston). "Duolingo for screen time": blocking sessions (solo, group, Beast Mode), streaks with optional money stake, weekly friend leaderboards, points for real rewards (Headspace, ClassPass), and the line "25 years. That's the average time you will spend on your phone" (verified). Onboarding 32 steps with a "shock and reframe" sequence, soft paywall, single annual plan with 7-day trial (3P). Prices: annual $24.99 to $59.99 (several trial lengths), monthly $3.99 to $14.99, family $99.99 (verified). 97K ratings, 4.81, about 362K MAU, 1.1K new ratings per week (3P); $150K to $400K/month (ScreensDesign, conflicting 3P figures); "over 500,000 people" (self-reported). I found no funding data.

**Jomo** (iOS, Mac; Oct 2022, France). Rule-based blocking with intent prompts and mindful breaks. Prices: $5.99/month, $29.99/year, lifetime $84.99 to $99.99, family $59.99/year (verified). 8.2K ratings, 4.75, about 29K MAU (3P). Complaints: conflicts with other Screen Time apps, apps stay blocked after uninstall, date-change bypass (vendor help pages, review).

**Forest** (iOS, Android, browser; May 2014, Seekrtech). Plant a tree during a focus session; leave and it withers; rooms for co-focusing; real trees planted. Onboarding 12 steps, soft paywall (3P). $3.99 upfront historically; now annual $35.99, monthly $5.99 plus coin IAP (verified). 565K ratings, 4.81, about 3.3M MAU (3P); "60 million people" (self-reported). Complaints: dead trees cause guilt, does not block on other devices, widget breaks after iOS updates (review).

**Clearspace** (iOS; Jul 2021). Friction screens showing opens versus goal, optional push-up unlock, "Teammates" social feature. Onboarding 7 steps, soft paywall, single annual plan with 7-day trial (3P). Prices: $6.99/month, $44.99 to $59.99/year, family $79.99 (verified). 12K ratings, 4.70, about 27K MAU, 69% ad revenue (3P). Complaints: lag of several seconds per tap (review).

**Roots** (iOS; Aug 2023, owned by MWM). App limits, downtime, daily goals, a "balance score." Prices: $7.99/week, $9.99/month, $29.99 to $59.99/year, $99.99 premium (verified). 4K ratings, 4.75, about 20K MAU (3P).

**Breakouts worth watching.** *Focus Friend by Hank Green* (Jul 2025): a bean knits while you focus and is sad if you quit; hit No. 1 free in the US App Store on 19 Aug 2025 (press); 14K ratings, about 88K MAU (3P), $350K/month revenue estimate (ScreensDesign, 3P); Pro $19.99/year, lifetime $39.99 (verified). Proves a lovable character plus creator audience can top the charts, but it is a focus timer, not a daily blocker. *Unglue* (Jan 2026): earn screen time by completing habits; about 2.7K US ratings, 110K installs (3P). *Friend Controls* (Sep 2025): friends approve or deny extra time; 1.1K ratings, about 4.3K MAU (3P); yearly $14.99 to $44.99 (verified).

## Crowded versus open

**Crowded.** Plain scheduled blocking (Opal, Jomo, Roots, Clearspace, many clones); pause-before-open friction (one sec, ScreenZen, Clearspace, Brainrot); "earn your screen time" with exercise, prayer, flashcards or habits (Unglue, Pushscroll, prayer lock, Study G); the years-of-life shock stat (Brainrot, BePresent); long emotional onboarding into a trial paywall (everyone big).

**Contested but young.** Friend accountability. Two flavours exist. *Permission* apps where friends approve or deny extra time: Friend Controls, Snitch (group "time pot," $34.99/year individual, $69.99 group), Narc (Apr 2026), and now ScreenZen's partner unlock. *Visibility* apps where friends see you slip: TimeBack ("when you override a block, your friends can be notified," verified listing), Snitch (group sees unlocks), Screen Freedom. Plus *competition*: Opal and BePresent leaderboards. None of the dedicated friend apps has scale. Our "1 to 3 friends get a gentle note when you bypass" is the visibility flavour, the least taken of the three.

**Open.** (1) A character whose primary loop is recovery: health returns with good days, missed streaks can be earned back. Brainrot decays, Forest dies, Focus Friend is sad; none centres healing. (2) A student-first product: finals-season mode, study-hours counter, campus or dorm groups. Opal's student SKU is pricing, not product. (3) Honest billing as a stated feature: charge-date reminders and no exit-discount trickery. Brainrot's own reviews call its exit discount "scammy," which makes this a direct contrast.

## Does anyone already do our exact combination?

No, as of 6 Nov 2026 I found no app combining a character that wilts and heals, a "years back" counter, friends notified on bypass, and a student mode. Closest partial matches:
1. **BePresent**: years framing, friends, group sessions, streaks; no character, not student-specific. Biggest overlap and strongest momentum.
2. **Brainrot**: pet that decays, years-of-life onboarding, Gen Z tone; no friend features found in the listing.
3. **TimeBack** and **Snitch**: friends see your overrides; no character, tiny.
4. **Focus Friend**: lovable character, student-coded; focus timer, no daily screen-time health, no friends.

What I could not find: Brainrot's in-app social features beyond the listing (none are advertised), BePresent funding, verified revenue for any app other than via founders, and Android figures for most apps.
