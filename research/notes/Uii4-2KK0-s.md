## Core claims (3-7 bullets, what the video argues)
- (Video description) Short no-code-platform lesson listing "5 quick hacks" to reach 1M downloads.
- Appy Pie's written version: getting to a million installs "is not one viral moment, it is stacking the right tactics in the right order over months"; optimize the app and listing first, then organic, then paid.
- ASO is the highest-ROI sustained channel because most discovery happens via store search.
- Reviews (recent velocity, not total count) feed both ranking and conversion.
- Freemium dramatically raises installs; "even $0.99 of friction is measurable."
- Burst campaigns (24-72h concentrated paid spend) spike rankings and unlock organic installs, but only on an already optimized listing.

## Specific tactics (concrete, actionable; include numbers, prices, timings, exact UI choices)
- Listing: users decide in seconds based on icon, first two screenshots and star rating, in that order; add captioned screenshots and a 15-second preview video; launch on iOS and Android (and consider Huawei AppGallery / Samsung Galaxy Store).
- Reviews: prompt after the first meaningful action, never on first open; respond to every 1-2 star review within 24 hours (many re-rate); in-app referral reward per friend who downloads and opens.
- Media: press kit (icon, screenshots, preview, one-page metrics) pitched personally; offer exclusivity/early access/original data; short-form creators now drive more installs than tech blogs.
- Content engine: long-tail keyword posts ("how to make a restaurant app"), localize listings/posts (they did Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi, Arabic), <60s build-in-public clips.
- Deals/contests: 30-day referral contest with tiered prizes and unique invite codes, require a social share to enter; holiday limited-time offers. Contests drove the most installs per dollar.
- ASO: front-load primary keyword in name and subtitle; use all 100 iOS keyword characters, no spaces after commas, add synonyms; treat first two screenshots as ad creative; monthly in-app events.
- Freemium: useful free tier; sell upgrades (branding, analytics, premium templates); trigger upgrade prompt at a natural limit (e.g., after building 3 apps), not on a timer.
- Burst: $5K-$15K over 48-72h (test with $2-3K); weight Apple Search Ads first, then Google, then social; low-quality installs now hurt rankings.
