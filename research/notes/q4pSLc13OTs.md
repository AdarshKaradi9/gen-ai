# I Studied 100 Viral AI Apps, Here's How They Market

- Confidence: high

## Core claims (3-7 bullets, what the video argues)
- After studying 100+ AI apps doing millions in MRR: winners don't have the best product, code or most features; "the best apps are winning because of their marketing approach, and they all follow one general method."
- The method: short-form organic content first -> test winners as paid ads -> rinse and repeat, with armies of creators (UGC, influencers, AI).
- Pre-condition: a strong in-app conversion mechanism, because "the more money you earn per user, the more you can spend on paid ads."
- Market research before any content: only learn from apps making >$50k/month (Sensor Tower), then mine their organic and paid content.
- Content should sell the outcome, not the features; CTAs should be "sneaky".
- Scale with creators paid on results (CPM / view guarantees), then paid ads optimized for money events, governed by CAC < LTV.

## Specific tactics (concrete, actionable; include numbers, prices, timings, exact UI choices)
- Funnel framing: ads/content -> App Store listing conversion -> onboarding with "positive friction" -> A/B-tested paywall. Fix this before spending on marketing.
- Market research tools: Sensor Tower (ignore apps <$50k/mo revenue); Viral Ads Library (organic + paid by niche); TikTok/Instagram keyword search (e.g., "dating tips" surfaces 426k/300k/115k-like videos that are low-effort talking-head "story time" formats you can plug a product into); TikTok Top Ads dashboard (US > Apps > category, last 30/180 days); Facebook Ads Library (many active competitor ads = positive signal). Log everything in a spreadsheet: URL, likes, views, comments, reshares, transcript.
- Creator scaling: start posting yourself (faceless OK); hire creators on results-only basis; CPM campaigns via his product Posted (set a CPM, own the videos and Spark Ads codes, track first app opens); bigger influencers work on view guarantees; keep a consistent team and ride a winning format "until it doesn't work anymore"; repost every video to TikTok, Reels, Shorts, Facebook Reels.
- Profile optimization: repost all creator content on your own account; playlists showing how to use the product; clean bio with App Store link; pin the videos that drove the most downloads (ReciMe doesn't do this; he does on Puff Count and Posted).
- Content optimization: "sneaky" CTAs, never a sales pitch; reply to comments with videos (Puff Count's 8.4M-view video was a reply to a comment: took apart a vape, then "this app helps you quit"); Cal AI ad example: jacked guy eating a healthy meal, product placement only.
- Paid ads: start once you have 20+ high-quality organic pieces; upload top organic performers; optimize campaigns for money events (trial start / subscription), not clicks or installs; watch CAC vs LTV; read realized LTV per install in RevenueCat.

## Named examples (apps/companies cited and what they did)
- Puff Count (his quit-vaping app): scaled to $45k MRR; LTV per install ~$1.00-1.20, max ~$1.50; CPI kept below that by mass creative testing.
- Posted (his current company, postedapp.com): >$1.3M in sales; CPM creator marketplace.
- ReciMe: $500k/month, 300k downloads last month; TikTok profile reposts creator content.
- Cal AI: $2M last month; lifestyle-style ad with product placement.
- Viral Ads Library, Sensor Tower, TikTok Ads Library, Facebook Ads Library, RevenueCat.
