# I made $800k building silly apps (this is how)
- URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3sLP2bcD5Xg | Channel: Adam Lyttle | Views: 141231 | Length: 451s (~7.5 min)
- Sources used: https://x.com/adamlyttleapps/status/1929733455728034074 (Adam's own $800k milestone post); https://www.onemilliongoal.com/p/adam-lyttle-the-man-who-builds-silly (long-form profile covering the $800k story and the paywall change); https://nocodeexits.substack.com/p/he-was-200k-in-debt-now-he-ships (profile of the app-a-month portfolio machine with pricing details); https://creators.spotify.com/pod/profile/adamlyttleapps/ (podcast descriptions incl. the 230% paywall episode and the 10-apps-in-30-days episode); https://provenstartups.com/projects/adam-lyttle-app-store-account-risk-portfolio and https://indiemakerlist.com/p/adam-lyttle/ (later-figure context)
- Confidence: medium (no transcript of this specific video; Adam's own posts plus two detailed profiles that retell the same story and numbers)

## Core claims (3-7 bullets, what the video argues)
- You do not need a big idea or investors: a portfolio of "silly" single-purpose apps, shipped roughly one per month, compounds into real money ($800K in cumulative App Store sales at the time of the video, self-reported).
- Let the App Store pick the ideas: keyword research for searches with demand and weak competition, build the smallest app that serves the search, ship, and let data decide which apps get more time.
- Distribution and monetization are separate problems; solve distribution first (volume + ASO), then monetization. Swapping in a high-converting paywall across an existing portfolio "changed everything".
- Small bets are cheap to lose and individually sellable; a few winners carry the rest.

## Specific tactics (concrete, actionable; include numbers, prices, timings, exact UI choices)
- Cadence: one new iPhone app roughly every month (started January 2020); with ChatGPT he challenged himself to build 10 SwiftUI apps in 30 days and shipped 9 (ChatGPT for code, Midjourney for icons).
- Idea selection: App Store keyword research for high-traffic, low-competition terms; build only for demand you can see; tools he names across his content include Astro and Appfigures.
- Stack evolution: HTML/JS/PHP -> Cordova -> SwiftUI; icons generated with a simple Midjourney prompt instead of hiring a designer.
- Monetization: freemium with weekly subscriptions around $4.99-$7.99 or an annual plan around $29.99 (per nocodeexits profile).
- The paywall switch: he studied an app doing ~$300K/month, copied its onboarding + paywall structure (refined onboarding, strategic free trial) and saw a 230% increase in trial subscriptions on his own app (podcast episode "I increased mobile app subscriptions 230% with one simple change"). Across the portfolio, monthly revenue went from ~$10K to ~$50K "essentially overnight" (onemilliongoal profile).
- Kill losers cheaply, pour attention into winners; buy and transform existing apps too (he bought at least one copycat app, rebuilt it).
- Build to keep, not to sell: after selling eight apps in two years (one sold for 2x annual revenue, $224K, on ~$11K/month) he switched to subscriptions so the portfolio compounds.
- Open-sourced his reusable components (onboarding flows, paywall screens, OpenAI wrappers) on GitHub so others can ship faster.

## Named examples (apps/companies cited and what they did)
- Adam's apps: Card Value Scanner for Pokemon, Fish Identifier, Insect Identifier, vegan ingredient scanner (three "AI wrapper" scanner apps), Intermittent Fasting+/Fast Tracker, Pushups Counter, iMic, Poop Tracker Deluxe, OB Wheel for Pregnancy, a lawn-mowing route app (his first; flopped after six months of work), a tarot app (early hit), later Swipe the Cat, Piano Run, Piano Pals, El Taxico, Draw Pop.
- Inspirations: an unnamed app generating ~$300K/month whose paywall he modeled.
- Tools: ChatGPT, Midjourney, SwiftUI, Astro, RevenueCat ecosystem.

## Numbers & evidence (any stats, revenue, conversion rates, retention figures; mark self-reported vs third-party)
- $800K cumulative App Store sales (self-reported, X post June 2025: "Reached $800k from App Store sales / Crazy how much impact a simple paywall made to my revenue"); earlier "$600k in consumer app sales" post; passed $1M cumulative in August 2025 (self-reported, indiemakerlist).

- One app at ~$11K/month sold for $224K (2x annual revenue); eight apps sold over two years (self-reported via profiles).
- Portfolio: 50+ apps built, 30+ active; ~$10K -> ~$50K/month after paywall/onboarding redesign; ~$70K/month by early 2026 (RevenueCat Launched podcast, self-reported); 2M+ downloads lifetime (self-reported, provenstartups).
- 230% increase in trial subscriptions from one paywall change (self-reported podcast episode title).
- 47K X followers (profile).

## Contradictions or caveats (where this disagrees with common advice or other videos)
- Volume is not the revenue driver he is remembered for: profiles stress the jump came from monetization (paywall) after years of distribution work, not from the 50th app.
- "Build to sell" worked but left him at zero after each sale (the "Pepsi Max moment": could not afford a soft drink at a family dinner), prompting the switch to subscriptions.
- The same paywall style later "started getting rejected by Apple" (onemilliongoal), and his 2026 content says the launch-day organic boost is gone, keyword-chasing (e.g., "Nano Banana") got developers' accounts terminated, and he now prioritizes feature-worthy apps over many small tests. The playbook in this video is therefore dated by his own later admission.
- AI wrapper risk: after WWDC 2024 he publicly worried iOS 18's ChatGPT integration would make his three scanner apps obsolete.
