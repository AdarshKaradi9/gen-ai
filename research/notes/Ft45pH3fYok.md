## Core claims (3-7 bullets, what the video argues)
- Per the description: "the real game is not growth ... it is getting people to come back again and again." "Why 90 percent of startups fail even with great products"; "you can buy growth but you cannot buy engagement."

## Specific tactics (concrete, actionable; include numbers, prices, timings, exact UI choices)
- Chapter map (from description): 4:04 What makes a startup legendary; 5:50 Hook Model explained; 16:09 Limits of emotional triggers; 20:08 Choosing right triggers and timing; 24:48 How a fitness app used the Hook Model; 28:21 Habit vs routine difference; 33:07 Variable rewards framework; 41:38 Why most people fail; 44:41 Why limiting beliefs form; 53:58 Can you change beliefs; 1:10:26 Closing.
- Hook Model mechanics as Eyal writes them (assumed to be what is explained at 5:50): pair external triggers with an internal emotional trigger; make the action the simplest behaviour done in anticipation of reward; use variable rewards (unpredictability drives wanting); ask for an investment (data, content, followers, reputation, skill) that loads the next trigger and improves the product.
- Variable rewards framework (33:07): in Eyal's book this is rewards of the tribe (social), the hunt (material/information) and the self (mastery/completion) - the sources read here do not confirm which were discussed on the show.
- Habit vs routine (28:21): Eyal's usual distinction is that a habit is done with little or no conscious thought, while a routine requires deliberate effort - unverified for this episode.
