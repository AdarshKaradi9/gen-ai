## Core claims (3-7 bullets, what the video argues)
- Diego Roshardt built AppAlchemy (AI tool that generates mobile app designs) to $17K MRR in ~4 months with zero audience (<100 X followers, no YouTube, no TikTok) and $0 marketing spend, purely through Reddit.
- You do not need an audience to grow a SaaS; go where buyers already cluster (subreddits) instead of spending a year building a following.
- Reddit works because of free organic reach plus precise targeting via thousands of niche subreddits.
- Solve a problem you have personally paid for (he paid an Upwork designer hundreds of dollars and was charged for every revision) in a growing market (vibe coding), not a declining one (newspapers).
- Speed is the solo founder's edge: 2-week MVP, marketing on day one, boring popular tech stack so AI codes it well.

## Specific tactics (concrete, actionable; include numbers, prices, timings, exact UI choices)
- The Reddit playbook, step by step: (1) become an active Reddit user first to warm up the account and learn what performs (new accounts posting links get flagged); (2) build a list of ~30 relevant subreddits using the Reddit Ads targeting tool as a free discovery tool (search a keyword like "no code" and let it suggest communities), no spend required; (3) write a value-first post: catchy headline that does NOT announce a launch, then genuine value (he summarized a successful mobile-app case study from Twitter), then plug the product mid-post; include a direct link only where rules allow, otherwise mention it alongside a complementary tool; (4) cross-post the same piece across 10+ subreddits; (5) iterate formats (case studies, videos, tutorials, discussion posts).
- Posting cadence: 2-3 posts per week to stay under spam thresholds.
- Volume math: "post on 10 different subreddits and each gets on average around 10k views — that's a total of 100k views already."
- Do not lead with the product, list features, or link a Discord in the post.
- Stack: Cursor ($20/mo), Vercel ($40/mo), Next.js/React, Firebase, Chakra UI and Ant Design component libraries; AI API backend ~$2,500/mo; ~70% margins.
- Pricing: $20/month.
- Time allocation: spend most early time on marketing instead of "adding more features no one asked for."

## Named examples (apps/companies cited and what they did)
