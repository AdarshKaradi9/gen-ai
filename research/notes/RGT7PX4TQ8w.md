# My Tech Stack For Building Viral Apps ($40K/month SaaS)

## Core claims (3-7 bullets, what the video argues)
- Steven scaled Puff Count (quit-vaping tracker) to ~$40-44K MRR and sold it; the tools that mattered map to four phases: Research, Building, Optimization, Marketing.
- Most founders skip research and jump to building; research "made the difference between Puff Count succeeding and failed apps dying at 100 downloads."
- Most founders also skip optimization and go straight from building to marketing, which is why revenue "sucks"; paywall and funnel testing is where revenue scaled.
- Marketing is the bulk of success (wiki paraphrase of his thesis: 90-95%); you need a repeatable organic content system, not one-off virals, then scale winners with paid.
- "Tools alone won't build your app business. You need a system for using them at the right stage."

## Specific tactics (concrete, actionable; include numbers, prices, timings, exact UI choices)
- Research: start from problems in your own life; Google Trends to confirm search volume is rising, not falling ("quit vaping" was his trend); Sensor Tower to see whether competitors make money.
- Building: sketch all features/flows/interfaces on paper first; 99designs design contests (pay only the winner); Upwork for devs/marketers; Mobbin to study top apps' onboarding flows; Paywall Screens (paywallscreens.com) to see which paywalls convert.
- Optimization: Superwall to split-test paywalls remotely without App Store updates (third-party source: took Puff Count from $3K to $30K MRR; price tests in the $4-$12 range per a search-snippet summary); RevenueCat for LTV/subscription tracking; Mixpanel/Amplitude for drop-off; AppsFlyer for attribution of TikTok/Meta paid ads. "A 0.5% conversion lift = real dollars."
- Marketing: organic TikTok/Instagram, posted daily, studied what went viral and recreated winning formats; Posted (his own product) to run creator contests that yield hundreds of videos with no upfront cost, paying only for winners; TikTok/Meta Ads Manager to scale winning organic creative.
- Wider system (wiki): long onboarding + hard paywall, A/B test in Superwall, scale on paid at ~1:3 CAC:LTV, push the yearly plan to maximize LTV.

## Named examples (apps/companies cited and what they did)
- Puff Count: $44K MRR (peak), ~1M users, exited to a European app studio.
- Posted (postedapp.com): "99designs for TikTok content," results-only creator marketplace; wiki cites $150K+/month.
- Tools named: Google Trends, Sensor Tower, 99designs, Upwork, Mobbin, Paywall Screens, Superwall, Mixpanel, Amplitude, RevenueCat, AppsFlyer, TikTok/Meta Ads Manager.

## Numbers & evidence (any stats, revenue, conversion rates, retention figures; mark self-reported vs third-party)
- Self-reported: $40K MRR (video title) / $44K MRR (newsletter); daily TikTok posting.
- Third-party: $3K to $30K MRR attributed to Superwall paywall testing; $4-$12 price test range; 1M users; Posted $150K+/mo and $1.3M+ total.

## Contradictions or caveats (where this disagrees with common advice or other videos)
- Advocates a long onboarding and hard paywall with high prices, the opposite of Hunter Isaacson's "tiny % pays, $1-$7 weekly" model.
- Newsletter and video titles disagree slightly on MRR ($40K vs $44K).
- Content is partly a funnel for his accelerator and his own tool (Posted); tool recommendations are not neutral.
