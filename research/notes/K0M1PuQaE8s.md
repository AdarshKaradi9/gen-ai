## Core claims (3-7 bullets, what the video argues)
- Casino techniques have "made their way into gaming" and changed the types of games made; the industry (now out-earning music and Hollywood) has shifted over the past decade to capitalise on it.
- Loot boxes / microtransactions ("surprise mechanics according to EA") are only the visible symptom; regulation of loot boxes is "a bandaid solution to a much broader problem."
- The same techniques used to make loot boxes addictive are being used as a central component of game design: games are "not only ... designed to get you to spend more but they're also deliberately trying to get you addicted."
- Mechanisms named: psychological manipulation via positive reinforcement and ratio scheduling (variable-ratio reinforcement), conditioning players into extended sessions - "a form of addiction that has many parallels to gambling and substance abuse" that is "almost completely unregulated."
- Minors and people with addiction problems are the ones exposed; the fix is education, calling out predatory design, and voting with your wallet for studios "doing the right thing."
