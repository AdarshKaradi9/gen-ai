## Specific tactics (concrete, actionable; include numbers, prices, timings, exact UI choices)
- From the related Tim Gabe video (not confirmed for this one): offer a weekly plan first ("ask for a coffee", e.g. $7/week) rather than a $38-$400/year annual commitment on first contact; the annual upsell comes later once the user is "in deep".
- Put a dedicated 1-3 screen trial explanation before the paywall: say exactly when the charge happens, promise a reminder before trial end, headline copy "No payment due now" (Cal AI example).
- Test the number of plans shown and the trial structure before testing price; localize paywall language and currency.
- Add one explicit line on the paywall stating when and for what the user will be charged (Blinkist redesign).
- Run many paywall experiments per year (top performers ~15/year) instead of treating the paywall as a checkout screen you never touch.
