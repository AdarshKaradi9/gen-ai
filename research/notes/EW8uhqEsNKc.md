## Core claims (3-7 bullets, what the video argues)
- Life can be run as an RPG inside Notion by applying Yu-kai Chou's Octalysis framework (8 core drives) to daily tasks, habits and goals.
- Start with "epic meaning and calling": defining purpose gives direction the way a game's main quest does.
- Ownership drives engagement: a character with level, EXP, coins, HP, goal completion rate and a skill tree makes you "feel attached to your progress".
- Good habits earn XP; bad habits cost HP; a coin marketplace lets you buy real rewards; penalties (loss of coins/levels, "death") exploit loss aversion.
- Short feedback loops ("numbers go up right after a task") are addictive when the numbers map to meaningful value.
- Community/social elements (built-in chat, friendly competition and envy) are part of the design.

## Specific tactics (concrete, actionable; include numbers, prices, timings, exact UI choices)
- Character sheet in Notion: level, EXP, coins, HP, goal completion rate, skill tree (skills level up as related habits/projects are completed).
- Habit tracker split into "Growth" (good habits, earn XP/coins) and "Fight" (bad habits, lose HP/coins); habit objectives such as "drinking 7 cups of water upgrades your Health skill".
- Task/project management uses the PARA method (Projects/Areas/Resources/Archives) reframed as quests and missions; long-term "areas" map to skills.
- Market / reward system: earn coins by completing tasks; spend them in a Market, Hotel, or Black Market on real-life rewards; proposed overdraft penalty when the coin balance goes negative.
- Penalties: missed check-ins or neglected habits cost progress; losing all HP triggers a "death penalty" that wipes coins, levels and XP.
- "AI Value Recommendation" (RPGenie) suggests balanced XP/coin rewards for a goal; "Companion Hall" NPCs assign daily/weekly/monthly quests with random rewards (unpredictability drive).
- Community: built-in chat room inside Notion to share progress; product launched on Product Hunt.
- Setup claim: "Set-up new account in 5 minutes"; works on Notion free plan; themes (Pixel RPG, Kawaii, Cyberpunk, Studio Ghibli), mobile version.

## Contradictions or caveats (where this disagrees with common advice or other videos)
- Leans heavily on black-hat drives (HP loss, "death penalty", overdraft penalties) that Chou's own TEDx talk warns are unsustainable long-term.
- Blog post's list of 8 drives is garbled (lists "Avoidance", "Scarcity", "Loss & Avoidance" and "Curiosity" separately, omits Ownership/Creativity), so use Chou's original for the framework.
- A system this elaborate (finance, sleep, mood, relationships) risks the "too many steps" trap called out in onboarding research.
