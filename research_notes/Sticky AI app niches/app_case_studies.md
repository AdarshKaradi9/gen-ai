## Q1. Retention mechanics and scale of sticky consumer apps (Strava, Headspace, Calm, Noom, Opal, Forest, Habitica, Locket, Brilliant, Pokémon GO, NYT Games, Snapchat, Robinhood)

### Takeaway
The stickiest apps tie a daily ritual to **social proof or loss aversion** (Strava kudos and clubs, Snapchat streaks, Locket's home-screen widget, NYT's bundle) or to **real-world progress** (Forest's real trees, Pokémon GO's walking and events). Public, audited numbers exist only for listed companies (Snap, NYT). Everything else is a third-party estimate. The meditation incumbents (Headspace, Calm) shrank their staff in 2022–2024 and turned to AI companions (Ebb), B2B and spin-off apps. Noom pivoted from coaching to GLP-1 prescriptions.

### Inferences
- Paywalling a beloved recap is a live tactic (Strava YIS, Dec 2025). Backlash came, but no churn evidence was published. A "year in review" earns its keep both as a share artifact and as an upsell trigger.
- Selling forgiveness for streaks is now an established product category: Snapchat sells tiers up to $4.99, alongside Duolingo freezes covered in the other notes. Paid streak repair is normal for consumers, but regulators (Massachusetts vs Robinhood) are hostile to gamification in **financial** contexts. Avoid confetti-style mechanics in money or health-treatment apps.
- Real-world tie-ins (Forest's 2M+ trees, Pokémon GO's walking) give the user a reason that outlives the novelty.
- The incumbents in meditation (Headspace) and weight loss (Noom) moved from content and coaching subscriptions toward **AI companions plus clinical or B2B services**. That suggests pure consumer content subscriptions plateaued (Calm's subscriber count fell from 2022 to 2023, per estimates).
- Locket shows the home-screen widget as a retention surface. Gen Alpha uptake of a weekly ritual (Rollcall) worked where daily posting fatigues users.

- Calm's 2024–2026 layoffs: none found. The Feb 2026 Calm price rise is single-source.

## Q2. Competitor pricing for couples, sleep, journaling and habit apps (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
Couples apps cluster at **$30–75/yr**, with one subscription shared by both partners. Consumer sleep apps run **$40–80/yr**, and CBT-I programs cost far more ($60/mo up to about $449). Journaling ranges from about $35/yr (Day One) to about $108/yr (Rosebud with AI memory), and AI tiers carry a clear premium (Stoic about $100/yr AI tier). Habit trackers are cheap ($5.99 one-time to about $35/yr) with **lifetime** options common, so a pure habit tracker has thin pricing power.

## Q3. Evidence on "AI coach that remembers you" products (Rosebud memory, Pi's decline, ChatGPT memory)

### Takeaway
Memory is now a **horizontal default**. Since April 2025 ChatGPT references all past chats (Plus and Pro), and ChatGPT Health keeps its own compartmentalized health memory with Apple Health and medical-record connectors. Pi, a general "personal AI" with about 6M MAU at peak, failed to find a business model and was capped after Microsoft hired away its team. Vertical apps such as Rosebud and Headspace's Ebb justify a subscription with **domain structure plus memory**, not memory alone.

### Cited Findings
- [HARD, press] **Pi / Inflection**: In Mar 2024 Microsoft hired Mustafa Suleyman and most of the staff, and Inflection pivoted to enterprise. Pi was dropped from WhatsApp and Messenger (May 2024). Free usage was rate-capped (Aug 2024) to save GPUs, and conversation export was offered. Suleyman said Pi had >1M active users but no "effective business model". Usage figures: about 1M DAU and 6M MAU in early 2024. — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2024/08/26/inflection-pi-ai-chatbot-enterprise); [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.in/tech/news/inflections-implosion-and-chatgpts-stall-reveal-ais-consumer-problem/articleshow/108648459.cms); [Bloomberg 2025](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-03-20/how-microsoft-lured-inflection-ai-s-staff-to-abandon-the-startup); [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2024/03/19/microsoft-surprise-deal-inflection-ai-mustafa-suleyman-reid-hoffman-questions/amp/)
- [HARD] **ChatGPT memory**: On Apr 10, 2025 ChatGPT began to "reference all of your past chats" for Plus and Pro users. BGR notes it stores key info rather than full transcripts. — [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/04/10/chatgpt-memory-improvements); [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/chatgpts-improved-memory-means-it-has-an-easier-time-remembering-your-previous-chats); [Engadget (2024 initial memory)](https://www.engadget.com/chatgpt-is-getting-a-digital-memory-to-recall-your-past-conversations-195126580.html)
- [HARD, OpenAI help] **ChatGPT Health** is a separate space. Health chats, memory and files stay walled off from the rest of ChatGPT. It connects Medical Records, Apple Health and supported wellness apps, with third-party apps off by default. — [OpenAI Help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001036-health-in-chatgpt)
- [HARD] **Rosebud** long-term memory is on by default for paid Bloom subscribers ($107.99/yr). - No source was found that explicitly assesses ChatGPT memory as a threat to vertical coaching apps. That framing is inference only.

### Inferences
- Pi's fate suggests that general-purpose empathetic chat with memory is a commodity that the platforms (ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot under Suleyman) absorb. Pi lost to cost and the lack of a business model, not to a lack of users.
- A vertical "coach that remembers you" must offer what ChatGPT does not: structured programs and streaks, domain data (wearables, logs), accountability rituals, human escalation, and B2B or insurance channels (the Headspace path). Otherwise ChatGPT Health plus memory is a free substitute.
- Price evidence (Q2) shows consumers still pay a 2.5–3x premium for AI-plus-memory journaling, so the moat holds for now, but it is narrowing.

- No Ebb usage metrics or Rosebud paid-subscriber counts from reliable sources. The existing notes hold a low-reliability estimate of about 7.5K paid users.
- No analyst or press piece directly addressing ChatGPT memory versus wellness apps was found in this pass.
