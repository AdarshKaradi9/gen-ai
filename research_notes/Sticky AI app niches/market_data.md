Access date for all sources: 2026-11-08.

Method note: in this session WebFetch could not reach revenuecat.com, a16z.news, or sensortower.com because they are outside the session's allowed network domains. Every finding below comes from search-engine result excerpts, which include primary pages such as RevenueCat, Sensor Tower press releases, Appfigures, SEC/Duolingo IR, and TechCrunch, plus secondary write-ups. Numbers that came through secondary write-ups should be checked against the primary PDFs before anyone relies on them.

Labels:
- **[HARD]**: measured data from a panel, company filing, or analytics vendor. Vendor revenue figures are model-based estimates, but they are systematic.
- **[EXPERT]**: analyst or VC opinion.
- **[SELF-REPORTED]**: founder or company claims that have not been audited.
- **[AGG-EST]**: aggregator estimates such as GetLatka, Sacra, or "spy" tools, which are lower confidence.

## 1. RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps (2025 and 2026 editions): category benchmarks, conversion, retention, pricing, paywalls, AI apps

### Takeaway
Health & Fitness is a top-tier subscription category. It has the highest annual-plan renewal rates, with Productivity close behind, a high trial-to-paid rate (~40% median), and most of its revenue on annual plans. Hard paywalls earn about 8x more revenue per install than freemium at day 60, with similar year-one retention. AI apps earn ~41% more per payer but churn ~30% faster. The median app earns very little: only ~4.6% reach $10K MRR within 2 years.

### Cited Findings
**Scale and sample**
- [HARD] A secondary summary describes the 2025 report as based on ~115,000 apps and ~$16B in revenue. — [SaaStr summary](https://saastr.com/the-top-10-learnings-from-revenuecats-state-of-subscription-apps-how-115000-mobile-apps-deliver-16b-in-revenue-whats-working-whats-quietly-killing-growth). The edition attribution comes from the URL slug and is unverified.
- [HARD] The 2026 edition is live at the primary URL. — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps). The 2025 edition is at [RevenueCat SOSA 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025).

**Trial conversion**
- [HARD] 2025 edition, median trial-to-paid by category: Travel 48.7% (highest), Media & Entertainment 43.8%. — [RevenueCat SOSA 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025)
- [HARD] 2025 edition, Health & Fitness trial-to-paid: median ~39.9%, top decile ~68.3%. This comes from a third-party summary of RevenueCat data. — [Singular summary](https://www.singular.net/blog/subscription-apps/) and [vmobify](https://vmobify.com/blog/free-trial-conversion-rate)
- [HARD] 2026 edition: trials of 17–32 days convert at a 42.5% median, against 25.5% for trials under 4 days, which is about 70% better. — [RevenueCat 2026 in 10 minutes](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026) and [RevenueCat free-trial-length blog (17,000+ apps)](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/free-trial-length)
- [HARD] 2025 edition, download-to-trial: high-priced apps have a 9.8% median against 4.3% for low-priced apps. A higher price does not suppress trial starts. — [RevenueCat SOSA 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025)

**Hard paywall vs freemium**
- [HARD] Download-to-paid with a hard paywall: 12.11% median against 2.18% for freemium. This is the 2025-era figure. — [arpubrothers 2025 summary](https://arpubrothers.com/blog/2025-saas-mobile-apps-trends/)
- [HARD] 2026 edition: hard paywall 10.7% download-to-paid by day 35, against 2.1% for freemium. **This conflicts with the 12.11% above.** The likely explanation is a different edition or measurement window. — [buildmvpfast summary of RC 2026](https://www.buildmvpfast.com/blog/hard-paywall-vs-free-trial-revenuecat-indie-app-2026) and [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)
- [HARD] Revenue per install, 2026 edition: hard paywall $2.32 against freemium $0.27 at day 14, and $3.09 against $0.38 at day 60 (about 8x). — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026 / Education page](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026-education)
- [HARD] 2026 edition: freemium and hard-paywall apps have nearly identical year-one retention, 28% and 27%. — [RevenueCat 2026 in 10 minutes](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026)
- [HARD] Fitness-specific paywall benchmarks are discussed in [RocketShip HQ](https://www.rocketshiphq.com/paywall-optimization-fitness-apps/) and [PaywallPro, 1,200 fitness paywalls](https://dev.to/paywallpro/how-top-fitness-apps-price-convert-insights-from-1200-paywalls-2p1d). I did not extract specific numbers from these.

**Retention and churn**
- [HARD] 2025 edition: nearly 30% first-month churn, and 36% one-year retention for cheap annual plans. Weekly plans retain uniformly poorly. — [RevenueCat SOSA 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025) via search summary
- [HARD] Median annual-plan retention fell from 47.1% to 44.1% (2025-era write-up). — [arpubrothers](https://arpubrothers.com/blog/2025-saas-mobile-apps-trends/)
- [HARD] 2026: Health & Fitness (86.4%) and Productivity (85.6%) lead "yearly retention," both above 85%. **Definition caution:** this cannot match the ~44% annual retention above. It is probably a renewal-rate or a different cohort metric, so verify it on the [RevenueCat renewal-rates-by-category blog](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/average-subscription-renewal-rates-by-app-category).
- [HARD] 2026: month 1 accounts for 35% of all annual-plan cancellations. Annual subscribers rarely return after they cancel. — [9to5Mac on RC 2026 part 2](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/27/new-report-shows-annual-app-subscribers-rarely-return-after-they-cancel/)

**LTV by category**
- [HARD] 2026: realized LTV per payer after year 1 is $24.95 for Productivity and $22.82 for Education (mid-tier). The best Business apps exceed $120. — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026 Education](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026-education)
- Gap: I found no Health & Fitness, Lifestyle, or Photo & Video RLTV figures in the search excerpts.

**Pricing and plan mix**
- [HARD] Health & Fitness, 2025-era: median annual $29.65, about 3.8x the $7.73 monthly. — [arpubrothers](https://arpubrothers.com/blog/2025-saas-mobile-apps-trends/)
- [HARD] Health & Fitness, 2026: pricing clusters at $39.94/yr and $9.99/mo, which points to price inflation year over year. — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps) via search summary
- [HARD] Share of Health & Fitness revenue from annual plans: **conflicting**. Figures cited are 59% (RevenueCat), 60.6% (Adapty), and 68% (RevenueCat per another write-up). — [Airbridge](https://www.airbridge.io/en/blog/weekly-vs-annual-subscription-app); [Medium, RC+Adapty](https://chuvak-pavel.medium.com/state-of-app-monetization-2026-key-trends-from-revenuecat-and-adapty-8bdc23a4bb6f)
- [EXPERT] Typical market price points are $4.99–6.99/week, $7.99–9.99/month, and $29.99–39.99/year. — [Airbridge](https://www.airbridge.io/en/blog/weekly-vs-annual-subscription-app)

**AI apps**
- [HARD] 2026: AI-powered apps earn about 41% more revenue per payer than non-AI apps but churn about 30% faster. They concentrate overwhelmingly on monthly plans. — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps); [arpubrothers 2026](https://arpubrothers.com/blog/revenuecat-subscription-app-report-2026/)

**Long-tail reality**
- [HARD] The median app earns ~$72/month one year after launch. Only ~4.6% of apps reach $10K MRR within 2 years. — [Data Analysis Journal](https://dataanalysis.substack.com/p/2026-benchmarks-more-apps-less-quality); [DEV, "new apps get 3% of subscription revenue"](https://dev.to/axrisi/app-economy-2026-why-new-apps-get-3-of-subscription-revenue-3cj8)

### Inferences
- A daily-use self-improvement app placed in Health & Fitness gets the best annual renewal economics. The trade-off is that AI features add churn pressure, so the habit loop and streaks have to offset the ~30% faster AI churn.
- The data supports a hard paywall with an annual default and a longer trial (about 2–4 weeks rather than 3 days). Year-one retention is no worse with a hard paywall, and revenue per install is about 8x higher.
- Because AI apps skew monthly, pushing annual plans hard is a differentiator. It also matters because month 1 is where most annual-plan cancellations happen.

### Gaps
- I could not open the full PDFs. Lifestyle and Photo & Video category medians, category-level month-1 and month-12 retention, and an AI-specific trial-conversion figure were not captured.
- The conflicting 12.11% vs 10.7% hard-paywall conversion figures and the "yearly retention 86%" definition both need checking against the primary source.

## 2. a16z Top 100 Gen AI Consumer Apps (5th edition Aug 2025, 6th edition Mar 2026, 7th edition Oct 2026)

### Takeaway
The lists are now dominated by general assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude) and creative tools. Turnover is slowing: the 7th edition had only 11 newcomers, the fewest ever. Spending is highly concentrated among power users. Self-improvement and health apps are not prominent in the top 100 rankings. That leaves an opening in this space, but it also means the a16z lists don't offer much direct evidence about it.

### Cited Findings
- [HARD] 5th edition (Aug 2025): the mobile list had 14 newcomers, attributed to app-store crackdowns on "ChatGPT copycats." 22 of the top 50 mobile apps were from China. The education entry QANDA (an AI math helper) appeared on mobile. — [a16z 5th ed.](https://a16z.com/100-gen-ai-apps-5/) via search summary; [johncandeto summary](https://johncandeto.com/information-flow/research-the-top-100-gen-ai-consumer-apps-5th-edition-andreessen-horowitz)
- [HARD] Companions: Character.AI ranked #3 on web and #16 on mobile, averaging ~298 sessions per user per month. This figure comes from the 4th-edition era and is possibly stale. — [a16z Top 100 index](https://a16z.com/100-gen-ai-apps/)
- [HARD] 6th edition (Mar 2026): scope widened to any consumer product where GenAI is core, which brought in CapCut, Canva, and Notion. ChatGPT remained #1. Yandex Browser was reported at 71M MAU. — [a16z.news, March 2026](https://www.a16z.news/p/top-100-gen-ai-consumer-apps-march); [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/a16z-just-ranked-the-100-most-popular-ai-apps-heres-the-full-list-and-what-it-tells-us-/)
- [HARD] 7th edition (~Oct 5, 2026): a new revenue leaderboard built from YipitData U.S. card spend. The top five by spend are OpenAI, Anthropic, Canva, Superhuman, and Higgsfield. Only 7 companies appear on both the traffic and spend rankings: ChatGPT, Claude, Suno, Perplexity, Photoroom, Canva, and Notion. Only 11 newcomers made the list. — [a16z 7th ed.](https://a16z.com/100-gen-ai-apps-7/); [a16z.news 7th](https://www.a16z.news/p/top-100-consumer-ai-apps-seventh); [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/a16z-top-100-consumer-ai-apps-seventh-edition/); [toolnavs](https://toolnavs.com/article/2319-a16z-top-100-consumer-ai-apps-7th-edition-only-11-newcomers-but-paying-users-are)
- [HARD] 7th edition: the top 1% of payers average $903/month on consumer AI and make up 19.5% of observed spend (Aug 2026). 4.5% of U.S. panel consumers paid for ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude, up from 2.1% a year earlier. ChatGPT has more than 1B monthly mobile users. Claude web is #3. — [KuCoin news summary](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/a16z-releases-7th-edition-of-top-100-consumer-ai-apps-chatgpt-leads-claude-web-rises-to-third); [Medium, Adnan Masood](https://medium.com/@adnanmasood/wide-usage-shallow-depth-reading-the-consumer-ai-spend-distribution-6983d2def327)
- [EXPERT] a16z (Olivia Moore, Josh Elman) describe a "power-user economy" and say most consumer AI monetizes through subscription. — [a16z on X](https://x.com/a16z/status/2107120344561852598)

### Inferences
- Paying for AI is still a minority behavior: under 5% of U.S. consumers pay for the top three assistants. A vertical self-improvement app has to justify its price through outcomes (weight, fluency, habits) and not through "AI access," which general assistants increasingly provide.
- Few newcomers suggests the horizontal category has consolidated. Vertical, mobile-first, habit apps such as Cal AI and Speak grow beneath the top-100 traffic thresholds, so this list is a poor scouting tool for the niche.

### Gaps
- I could not open the primary a16z pages, so I couldn't capture category-level breakdowns for education, health, "vibe," or companion apps in the 6th and 7th editions.

## 3. Sensor Tower and Appfigures: State of Mobile, AI app revenue and downloads, Health & Fitness and Education

### Takeaway
2025 was the first year consumers spent more on (non-game) apps than games. AI apps had the fastest revenue growth (+254% YoY in Sensor Tower's framing), and Health & Fitness IAP reached ~$4.5B, with AI calorie trackers such as Cal AI among the top growers.

### Cited Findings
- [HARD] Sensor Tower State of Mobile 2026, covering 2025: 149B app downloads and $167B app IAP revenue (+10.6% YoY). Apps outspent games for the first time. AI app revenue grew +254%. AI downloads grew +148%. Short-drama downloads grew +278%, the fastest. — [Sensor Tower press release](https://sensortower.com/press/press-release-boosted-by-gen-ai-services-consumers-spent-more-money-in-apps-than-games-for-first-time); [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/01/21/sensor-towers-state-of-mobile-2026-tiktok-dominates-ai-apps-surge-games-lose-ground/); [Sensor Tower blog](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-mobile-2026)
- [HARD] Users spent 48B hours in AI apps in 2025, about 3.6x 2024. Mobile users discussed health and fitness more than work or productivity with AI assistants. — [DeepLearning.AI The Batch](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/state-of-mobile-2026-report-shows-ai-chatbot-search-and-assistant-growth-outpaces-gaming-social-and-more/)
- [HARD] Health & Fitness 2025: ~4B downloads and $4.5B IAP revenue, with AI features driving growth. AI nutrition and diet tracking (Cal AI, MacroFactor, YAZIO) ranked among the top IAP revenue growers. Strava and Runna also grew strongly. — [Sensor Tower, Health & Fitness AI](https://sensortower.com/blog/health-and-fitness-apps-ai)
- [HARD] Generative AI apps in 2025: downloads doubled to 3.8B and IAP revenue nearly tripled to more than $5B. — [Sensor Tower State of AI 2026 press](https://sensortower.com/press/sensor-tower-state-of-ai-2026-report-global-time-spent-on-generative-ai-apps-projected-to-more-than-double-year-over-year)
- [HARD] H1 2026 projections: AI-app IAP of more than $4B (+36% half over half). A broader cohort of apps mentioning AI terms is tracking ~$12B. GenAI time spent is projected at 36B hours, against 17.2B in H1 2025. GenAI downloads are projected at 2.3B (+7% HoH), which points to slowing download growth and rising monetization. ChatGPT hit 1B MAU in May 2026. — [Sensor Tower State of AI 2026 press](https://sensortower.com/press/sensor-tower-state-of-ai-2026-report-global-time-spent-on-generative-ai-apps-projected-to-more-than-double-year-over-year); [Morningstar/PRN](https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20260616sf83943/sensor-tower-state-of-ai-2026-report-global-time-spent-on-generative-ai-apps-projected-to-more-than-double-year-over-year)
- [HARD] Appfigures (Jan 2025): AI app gross monthly revenue grew 37x in two years. 2024 consumer spend was over $1.4B, and Appfigures projected more than $2B for 2025. "General Assistant / thin wrappers" took 40% of top-1K AI app spend since Jan 2023, and all of the top 10 earners were general assistants. 64% of spend came from the U.S. — [Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20250131?f=1). **Conflict:** Sensor Tower put 2024 GenAI app spend at about $1.1B. — [Digital Information World](https://www.digitalinformationworld.com/2025/02/ai-apps-boom-general-assistants.html)
- [HARD] Appfigures: ChatGPT mobile consumer spend reached about $2.5B in 2025. — [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/artificial-intelligence-2/2025/consumers-spent-2-5-billion-in-chatgpt-mobile-app-in-2025/)
- [HARD] Appfigures, AI companions: ~$82M in H1 2025, projected above $120M for 2025, and $221M lifetime as of July 2025. The top 10% of apps earned 89% of revenue. Revenue per download rose from $0.52 (2024) to $1.18 (2025). 128 of 337 revenue-generating companion apps launched in 2025. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/12/ai-companion-apps-on-track-to-pull-in-120m-in-2025)
- [HARD] Appfigures: new app launches rose about 60% YoY in Q1 2026. Utilities was #2, Lifestyle #3, and Productivity and Health & Fitness made the top 5 by launches. This points to rising supply and competition, possibly from AI-coded apps. — [AI Directory on Appfigures](https://aidirectory.com/news/appfigures-data-new-app-launches-jumped-early-2026)
- [HARD] Duolingo Q3 2026: DAU 64.9M (+29% YoY, accelerating from 23%), paid subscribers ~13.1M (+14%, decelerating), revenue $309.0M (+14%), bookings $316.1M (+12%), gross margin 72.9%. FY2026 revenue guidance is ~$1.22B (+18%). The Video Call rollout is pressuring gross margin through AI costs. — [Duolingo Q3 2026 shareholder letter](https://investors.duolingo.com/static-files/e1ee336e-b024-486f-875c-afd722653067); [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/duolingo-inc-duol-q3-2026-050948751.html)

### Inferences
- Health & Fitness ($4.5B IAP) is the largest self-improvement revenue pool where AI is measurably moving revenue, especially photo-based nutrition tracking.
- AI downloads are flattening (+7% HoH) while revenue and time spent keep compounding. The market is rewarding retention and monetization over novelty.
- The Duolingo data shows AI features (Video Call) can reaccelerate engagement, but inference costs eat into gross margin. That is a COGS consideration for any GenAI daily-use app.

### Gaps
- Sensor Tower's 2025 Education and Lifestyle category revenue totals, and a list of top-grossing self-improvement apps, were not in the excerpts. The State of AI 2026 report is gated.

### Inferences
- The proven AI-native wedge is "camera or voice input → instant personalized feedback → daily ritual." Cal AI (photo to calories), Umax (selfie to score), Speak and Praktika (voice to fluency), and Gauth (photo to answer) all fit this pattern.

## 5. Trends: faceless and small-team AI apps, rising categories, search trends, TikTok/UGC distribution costs

### Takeaway
Creator and UGC networks on TikTok and Reels are the documented growth engine for the breakout small-team apps (Cal AI, Umax). Hard cost-per-install data is not public. Category-specific trend evidence for menopause, ADHD, posture, and men's health is mostly expert opinion and not measured data.

### Cited Findings
- [SELF-REPORTED] Cal AI's growth came from a network of about 250 influencers posting monthly. Yadegari called it "mass spray and pray," with paid influencer content scaling the app. An agency case study claims 500+ creators, which is self-promotional. — [NetInfluencer interview](https://www.netinfluencer.com/cal-ai-founder-zach-yadegari-breaks-down-the-influencer-playbook-behind-his-apps-growth/); [theviralapp case study](https://theviralapp.com/portfolio/cal-ai/); [profitablefounder UGC playbook](https://www.profitablefounder.xyz/blog/hire-ugc-creators)
- [EXPERT] An AI-generated-UGC playbook for app growth in 2026 exists, but I extracted no cost figures from it. — [forkoff.xyz](https://forkoff.xyz/blog/founder-growth/ai-ugc-playbook-2026)
- [AGG-EST] Liven's growth was mostly paid ads. — [Growth Hacking Lab](https://thegrowthhackinglab.com/case-studies/liven-app-800k-revenue-1-year-old-app/)
- [ANECDOTE] Finch reportedly grew through an ambassador and peer-referral model. — [X @dailystartupfix](https://x.com/dailystartupfix/status/1921942935446175875)

### Inferences
- Organic and creator-led distribution, with high-volume and low-cost creator posts optimized for virality, is the repeatable channel for small teams. Apps whose core output is visual and shareable (calorie photos, face scores, before/after) fit it best.
- Rising launch counts in Health & Fitness and Lifestyle mean more competition. Differentiation will need to come from retention mechanics and not only from a novel AI feature.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable TikTok CPI or CPM benchmarks for subscription apps in 2025–2026, and no verified per-creator payout figures.
- No Google Trends evidence surfaced for looksmaxxing, calorie AI, menopause, ADHD, posture, or dopamine detox.
- Measured download or revenue data for menopause, ADHD, posture, and men's-health app categories was not found.
- I found no systematic data on indie "faceless" app revenue (for example, Indie Hackers or Starter Story aggregates).
